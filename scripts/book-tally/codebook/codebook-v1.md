# Book-tally codebook, v1

Status: **approved by the owner on 2026-11-18.** The machine-readable copy the coding uses is
`codebook-v1.json`. Any change makes a new version (v2), never an edit to v1.

## How every question is coded

For each video and each question, the reader (Haiku) answers in this order:

1. **Addressed?** Does the experiencer speak to this question in this account? *Yes* needs a quote
   copied word for word from the transcript. Code checks every quote. *No* (not addressed) needs
   no quote. Silence never counts as an answer.
2. **The answer.** What it looks like depends on the question's type:
   - **Yes/no**: *yes*, *no* or *mixed*, with a quote. "Yes means" below says exactly what *yes*
     stands for.
   - **Multiple choice**: one to three answers from the question's list, each with its own quote.
     *Other* (with a quote and a few words) catches anything not on the list. Because one person can
     give several answers, **the shares add up to more than 100%**. Each share reads "X% of those who
     addressed it mention this".
   - **Conditional**: the question is reported among people with a fact from the next section
     (for example, a violent death). It is also reported for everyone.
3. **People, not videos.** Answers are combined per person across all their videos, as now.

**What counts as an answer.** An answer counts when it is what the experiencer experienced, or
learned from their NDE, either during it or afterward through the understanding or connection it gave
them. It does not matter when in the account they say it. These don't count:
- beliefs they say they held only before the NDE (for example "I was raised Catholic");
- things that only an interviewer or host says;
- other people's accounts.

Answers are not tagged by when the person learned them.

**Rough frequency** (column ≈n): how many of the 6,054 videos the first run marked as addressing the
question. It is only a guide. That run coded either/or and open questions inconsistently, which is why
this redesign exists. Questions under about 30 are unlikely to give reportable figures.

## Facts about each account (coded once per video)

Used by the conditional questions, and available as a breakdown for any question. Each is *yes*,
*no* or *not stated*. *Yes* and *no* need a quote.

| Code | Fact |
|---|---|
| F0 | **What brought on the NDE** (multiple choice): illness · surgery or a medical procedure · cardiac arrest · accident · drowning · childbirth or pregnancy · violence (assault, stabbing, shooting, attempted murder) · suicide attempt · overdose or addiction · other |
| F1 | The NDE came suddenly, without warning (accident, sudden collapse), not during an expected or gradual decline |
| F2 | The experiencer was in severe pain or suffering just before the NDE |
| F3 | The experiencer was deeply depressed or very afraid just before the NDE |
| F4 | The experiencer was an atheist or nonreligious before the NDE |
| F5 | The experiencer had left the religion they were raised in before the NDE |
| F6 | All or part of the NDE was frightening or hellish |

## Decisions recorded (owner, 2026-11-18)

1. No tag for whether an answer came from during the NDE or later. The rule above,
   *What counts as an answer*, applies instead.
2. Rarely addressed questions are coded. Whether to report them is decided later.
3. Q24 is merged into Q22.
4. The question types, wording and answer lists above are approved.
5. Multiple-choice answers: up to three per person, so shares can exceed 100%. Reports must say so.
