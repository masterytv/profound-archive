## Tensions and contradictions

- **Loving afterlife versus hellish or void realms.** The overwhelming majority describe love, safety and homecoming (Nj6IFvWtlqM, PErf6g4FuTo, QVGA6xeQhOs, S6v6IjBZofk, VOBEOvAgm4I). Against this stand fiery, demonic or punitive settings (NqqoCcsQWQo, O1f0heJGsUM, OQ4tqcGsK-4, OjSv_natOLU, OY2aK2A-w7s, OupepTZCUZ0, QUpKuZNAaxA, R25f3zhQ4JM, S7T5qmE6jsU, SRe5KgTNzcs, UItXrmtyE28, Vhi6wFU46ec, VlR1eFIvdvY) and featureless voids with no dualities (QiW4rgle2Bk, QkEJCM6GId0, PdIXHExitkw, Ox-RqYQ2s9U). Many hellish accounts nonetheless end in rescue, light or service (Pdbj2oz58E4, QHNW29X-nrE, RNooA4tlBmU, TAdFR0DIrrI, T69fFcY-Puc).
- **Return as choice versus compulsion.** Chosen returns: NoQxPQ1Kjgc, O4dwEH8NU9I, Ot5byhyBYHM, PwKRiyk4PNQ, Qg29aKfNLIY, QqNIifpXSQs, RlcRpZZtVXE, SpyB-ElkeRk, Td0AAHSsz5E, VcnpKOC3KjI. Forced or commanded returns: NqXb7TNz0FI, O4s3K89w-fs, OKZWYCa7A3Y, OXUgsj1Dbho, Ob4hrE6KxwU, OswjIZagplI, P1MVSAwKZ8Y, PcUzlmOUIH4, QCIrW9ao3fM, QWs2qojUFoY, RzEYqGzDSfU, SbL70DvhseY, T5giGHYv4kU, ThW7SB7FmFA, U2QZu8pqc0I, VSz09b2NcvQ. A third mode returns for someone else's welfare rather than by command or preference: RrKHw7q0PvQ (a nurse's grief), S9NhDYQIRfQ, RZm22oA4o9A, RHdWN8AT7PY, Qc8rVIR19Pw. Some resist returning (S6GVCx0MpXc, UaXVshhUXYA, Ucdk2yR8WxQ, TueuzsvKDiE), and in SWRy_tpwsq8 a council, ancestors and a reaper-like entity negotiate the permission.
- **Evidential strength versus narrative richness.** High Veridical ratings often attach to sparse accounts (RgQmKWNP3DE with two elements, T6fEH_hoLh0 with five), while the most elaborate journeys are rated low (RJeL8me8zFM, RVuU5U_0beI, SnP4cbu3WxE, SsdHH5aOlus, U5ULZ5GUZJY, VOBEOvAgm4I, VPIOeSx6m2I). Greyson scores do not track intensity either: OTZVoRPfWkM has intensity 8 but Greyson 6, while O3cMj9h3oE4 has Greyson 30 and intensity 9. Many cards explicitly note no verifiable perceptions (OHzqvmqGh2s, OdspGKZmE4g, OglaN_u_5ms, Ot5byhyBYHM, OsMqrwiKwc8, PDHcU1yEKJY, PVeP9pwhD8E, QYoiJvEmvc8, QjSxTEAu22U, SGsDJUBbJIg, SZB-Z6zBaFM, TACMHun1Ogs, TlRdS1pFUuI), set against specific claims in OB8sJ4K6ytI, OQdS9iDDJzk, RBsPLFjGnVc, RJ0hTJGwf70, SVxQnMaPP20, TLeahFEj6iw, UEnE61R_aLI, V65jl0VCE4g, VbvV4etSRoY.
- **Christian specificity versus universalism, other pantheons and loss of faith.** Jesus, God and angels dominate O3cMj9h3oE4, QD-wTFOoI94, QS6gke39iiY, SnFdfEvy6L4, TlvIHuRZ-V4, R4YI-7rq01Y. Universalist or formless framings appear in OizX472ycCw (Buddha, Allah and Yahweh as one), QJepDqYO5iw (God as formless intelligence), QCIrW9ao3fM, Tebz0cidZds, V6DKsqTs9M8, RjlHo__Hsk8. Other pantheons surface in OL7R0ALJjN0 (Thoth), S9PQHOeqzmw and SK29hHyh0RQ (Buddha, Mother Tara, Shiva), T6fEH_hoLh0 (Shiva, Rishis), U8Ae4PMAAqk (Vishnu), TZUupVwc-Uk (27 archangels and Prophet Muhammad), RSm3eRtBKbc and RUwLLaaQP1E (Native and indigenous frames), O3Ff6Hc4O7Y (Jesus and Buddha together). Religious revision runs the other way: NmO3P8APlqU repudiates prior teaching, NwxLC_Rjgrc loosens from organized religion, Sfzj-xsHzkw reframes Christian belief, UCdzRHPGJzQ rejects simple religious framing, and VOBEOvAgm4I loses faith.
- **Tunnel and light versus instantaneous or dark arrival.** Tunnel or travel narratives appear in NoQxPQ1Kjgc, NyfUJD1xf08, QU2BrV2FqZE, Qd7mxjPtonE. Ns4NQiM1Stk explicitly reports no tunnel or travel, describing an instantaneous state beyond time; PRIMYkoZ9HY was "just immediately in the light"; VDm14xmdLd8 saw no bright light; UsYu5TYGgh0 and UfyGWa_fbss report no light or people, only a warm cosmic presence; RrKHw7q0PvQ enters peaceful blackness; QQy02qK9iq8 reports four tunnels to choose from.
- **Life review as compassion versus judgment, or absent altogether.** Nonjudgmental, empathy-centered reviews dominate (O5m1Z5hEnH4, OIo2kFjcWHs, Qb9SEatngv8, Qbvs1ncHcwo, SviA0uvGUq0, UntuKHaMpOI, UoPyL-cYGxw). Reviews framed through sin, punishment or shame appear in OJO8X1GK_ho, QmUQFufRj3c, S1Biml02x9E, Uf6jViTp2WA, UvZwj5acUJo, T-CQniGNTb0. VetWriAWgyQ describes no traditional life review and "no good or evil, just experiences and feelings"; ScCE1Y5v8u8 reports no emotional ties to body or family during the experience.
- **Immediate transformation versus delayed or minimal change.** Major transformation scores are common (P68Pb4RJUII, PBLEP5FkYw0, QXwyZoOf0xY at 42, RVuU5U_0beI at 43), but OtVY8UeVqUM, P95V4rsE1HE, Rx0gK67fqCo and S-7XkWCWwas record little or no change, and PqddJsxn16s says the experience had no initial impact and the experiencer remained an atheist for 35 years.
- **NDE label versus nonstandard material.** Clear sequences with high Greyson scores (Ojh4PQV4_MY, QuipTpfyfIA, UfYstzc8E3I) sit beside cards that are not usable NDE accounts: no transcript (O2whJPweTkQ), a compilation (OYv5k581Vdc), a historical summary of Swedenborg (PUW3DBLAJJk), low Greyson or no clear sequence (QytpIhdq8oY, RxMvSA-P0lE, Rva5kzXDy3A, Ut7qS1L8TNc, U0-6LEiJd7c with Greyson 0/32), a 1916 account via mediumship (RTXxA-Fm0qA), a moving-day transcript (S7-LOH8hVTE), and a transcript containing only "Hello." (TOvp2vvxjH8). The two Jacob Cooper cards give different ages, five in U8__UHT8hLI and three in UHm7IdnWoN4.
