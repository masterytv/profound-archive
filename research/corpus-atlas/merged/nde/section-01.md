Section nde-01 | batches: 001-020 | videos: ~800

## Themes

Counts are sums of per-batch estimates where a batch named the theme. Batches often folded related motifs together, so totals are approximate and overlapping.

1. **Peace, love, light, and homecoming.** approx. 560 of ~800. The dominant emotional register across every batch: unconditional love, radiant light, freedom from pain, and a sense of being home. Examples: -037JoSLNrM, 06RNZkRnevo, 0HGnO-tzeqw, 1oyZsqd0y3g, 5ZfaPCwjguk.
2. **Return as choice, command, duty, or mission.** approx. 505 of ~800. Experiencers are told, choose, or are compelled to return, most often for children, family, unfinished work, or a stated purpose. Examples: -1-uv1xfieg, -V_vgOuENrw, 1FD5lReqe64, 6BnbuAc1l_o, 7UY_9R02GKA.
3. **Spiritual transformation and reduced fear of death.** approx. 460 of ~800. Reduced fear of death, heightened spiritual awareness, compassion, and new purpose or vocation. Examples: -6B8UQ4isWE, 0OjYtaWv2HA, 2aCQi2Li8wc, 3aHSOCxbiuY, 5ycvUOwe2os.
4. **Out-of-body observation.** approx. 365 of ~800 (counted in 13 of 20 batches). Watching the body, the operating room, rescuers, or an accident scene from above, with widely varying evidential weight. Examples: -PiQsx1cuck, 0ATJGgBee1s, 1A_ehqvWZ_w, 3b7HhWuIL3Q, 6hogP2qF3RI.
5. **Deceased relatives, guides, angels, and named religious figures.** approx. 365 of ~800. Relatives as welcomers or return messengers; Jesus, God, angels, and guides as instructors. Examples: -DmWsmTFKsk, 0KepnGvqkII, 1TIktaxEJOk, 4HLgDKXe8TE, 6tBOQMYKDxE.
6. **Life review and moral consequence.** approx. 330 of ~800. Reviews that emphasize effects on other people rather than punishment, sometimes replaying harm from the victim's side. Examples: -7aF7tqghyg, 17vs21uAjVw, 2G3ixhMxb2I, 4BkS4DIiFP8, 7IIA8VnURZs.
7. **Unity and expanded consciousness.** approx. 40 of ~800 counted separately (batches 007 and 016), but folded into the love and peace theme nearly everywhere else. Merging with everything, Source, or a connected universe. Examples: 1RwMBZGXTIU, 1jUNek5gwnU, 5RSnbaTYtWY, 5hW_JoJY0WM, 6Oneto4uX7k.
8. **Hellish or distressing environments before rescue.** approx. 25 of ~800 counted in four batches (006, 015, 016, 019), but individual hellish accounts are named in every one of the 20 batches. Demons, darkness, cold, guilt, and impossible tasks, usually resolving through prayer or a figure of light. Examples: -I2ykWmNfPU, -wSVuwl0G4k, 1ehkaRi-0tE, 4_xBUvfnbyc, 6lDnttJnT08.
9. **Religious imagery beside nonreligious or metaphysical frameworks.** approx. 37 of ~800 counted separately (batches 009 and 010), but the contrast recurs in the tensions of most batches. Jesus and angels sit next to grids, gears, frequencies, Source, and libraries. Examples: 2TB8MIJn2dU, 2j3Y56wlR_E, 3GO8EulvT6g, 4ZPHunKGo3U, 6ZSULlSKEuo.
10. **Future visions, prophecy, and extraordinary knowledge.** approx. 10 of ~800 counted (batch 014), with named future-vision accounts in at least ten batches. Examples: -eg-kDs9-YI, 1SnKBa-ptHA, 37_GDecRdkA, 5HqwSwXU6VQ, 7N5wR77PfXA.
11. **Nonstandard experiences filed with NDEs.** approx. 8 of ~800 counted (batch 018), with many more named. STEs, shared death experiences, deathbed visions, ketamine and meditation states, research talks, and compilations. Examples: -4GjlmByWc8, -gKY-EmmTSQ, 3Guf-__HSzA, 4AmEiVvBQmo, 6ZSULlSKEuo.
12. **Childhood and infant NDEs.** approx. 6 of ~800 counted (batch 001), with named childhood accounts in most batches. Drowning is the most common trigger; memory is often reconstructed through family testimony. Examples: -831_OI42Qk, 0-O9HF9snQ8, 0mM3ut9pBvA, 3sIULqoDkFE, 6cviPcIr3GM.
13. **Evidential or veridical perception.** Not counted by the batches; batch 005 notes roughly 25 of its 40 cards rated Low Evidential despite high vividness. High-rated cases: -831_OI42Qk, 0SV5SeAo2ks, 2GqJWMn5kGs, 3F1SZEFhqco, 52e1gAdDH-Y.
14. **Multiple NDEs in one life.** Not counted; named in at least 16 accounts. Examples: 0I0Vcpcn5BE, 3Vh3vAJzZbE, 4fzw9Bgms5o, 5BeSkfVUvBw, 5nFjhZ2LaAU.
15. **Pre-birth memory and life planning.** Not counted; named in at least a dozen accounts. Examples: 0s03kVt2bcE, 4HvCz4ql1XE, 598sYF2OrPA, 6gw_dZD1eqI, 7PO-Op38o-k.
16. **Past lives and reincarnation.** Not counted; named in about a dozen accounts. Examples: -HtJPzoQ9fw, 00emCVLxnaU, 35IKZHvD9PY, 2_S7Ntgwxk0, 5gWvdXWjIek.
17. **Animals in the afterlife.** Not counted; deceased pets, animal guides, and animal-centered moral lessons appear in at least 18 accounts. Examples: -fVQdDqg4qk, 28o9qbvktN8, 4JqkeHdBwhI, 6Wq7PqKq5FQ, 7NE6bC506Ng.

## Tensions and contradictions

- **Positive welcome versus hellish realms.** The large majority describe peace and love (-831_OI42Qk, -BuIWy0cr4c, 1oyZsqd0y3g, 4f3NNz-3U2I, 6Oneto4uX7k), while a persistent minority begin in demons, darkness, cold, or torment before rescue (-Czk8zR_OeM, -I2ykWmNfPU, -QCo_coTkRE, -c79QpzNdhQ, 0HvoCmTBL7w, 0T7ZCIyfkSA, 0ewRlvXQAJc, 1ehkaRi-0tE, 2Dq1vB_ZOiM, 35GMgX3rQS0, 3U6a1aZgUwQ, 3bH946mWu18, 4_xBUvfnbyc, 4AX9t3d_AMk, 4ihBYcNIFV4, 5SGbWUWoP8Y, 5aRngG7_45w, 6unzquwnGzo, 6lDnttJnT08, 7Rp2RQrGAKo). 1ehkaRi-0tE begins in hell and remains distressing.
- **Is there a hell at all?** Hell is an actual realm in 5SGbWUWoP8Y, 5aRngG7_45w, 5oJbYGzDV3Q, and 78QEtegZ90g (seven levels organized by sin), while God explicitly teaches "there is no hell" in 5eDK4_MJFKI, 4DDJbEAm9q8 reports no duality of good and evil, and 3ByF8PVynOk describes a realm where everyone is welcome.
- **Choice versus compulsion in returning.** Chosen returns: -0qwThW5OJ4, -8jYSjXJdgk, -V_vgOuENrw, 1N7oh1KLcJ4, 2_S7Ntgwxk0, 3Z0udKDmfg8, 4icACL_02ro, 5--x0cNpeKo, 6PLpqoLeBrs. Commanded or forced returns: -67BdGnLSME, -DwyhIYLRFM, -UQxGKYsBHU, -h_5uX-Kd2Q, 0E5pdB69kao, 0R1tf931mqU, 1SnKBa-ptHA, 2dij8iX_f10, 3esgYXr9-LY, 4hXR4sd0INY, 4xx51AptzbM, 5mqTaStUURQ, 6jN-xtjkxs4, 7TfFeukn10I. Protest at being sent back: -pveKCy5OF8, -w16JTddbWA, 4PGlJU6TCcE. Reversal of refusal: 6GQDnIMpocU.
- **Veridical strength versus transformation.** Highly evidential cards often report modest aftereffects (-831_OI42Qk, -Bd3BAT50IM, 0ATJGgBee1s, 4g3qLiPMlCU), while the most transformative accounts often have low Veridical ratings (-6B8UQ4isWE, -EYve6oV45E, 5ZfaPCwjguk). High ratings do not always mean documented verification (1RmizfNKkYs, 1jyu35n9SnU), and vividness of 9 or 10 coexists with Low ratings (0Uuu5PgBqMo, 0rad5QROiLw). Infant cases break the instruments: 0mM3ut9pBvA scores Greyson 0/32 with High Evidential Strength.
- **Rich phenomenology versus minimal experience.** Fifteen-element cards (-7aF7tqghyg, -UQxGKYsBHU, 2TB8MIJn2dU, 22ge3Qp6sS8, 4BkS4DIiFP8, 5XrA79_T_R0, 69t5LgDucZk, 7_mSPxColVI) sit beside cards with one or two elements (-Bhv9YIRC-I, -Eo7FHLtYVA) and 5BeSkfVUvBw, which claims 60 NDEs but lists only two elements.
- **Life review as empathy versus life review as accusation.** Nonjudgmental reviews dominate (2G3ixhMxb2I, 3rEPSiujAyQ, 471XOH4Atwg, 5MjoNcVO-hE frames life as a game), but 2Oz98Aoaaf0 has accusers saying "You abandoned us when we needed you the most," 4728FuvkCOs reviews only harmful acts, and 4lCvDgftr7w and 4wf4v8Xa97o include anguishing reviews.
- **Christian specificity versus impersonal unity.** Jesus-centered accounts (-pveKCy5OF8, 003WkAP9aIU, 2aCQi2Li8wc, 4HLgDKXe8TE, 6sBoMtyRHMs) against Oneness, Quantum realm, Source, frequencies, and energy (-pbng7KjmXo, -nlW61RtmFE, 0YdrbmMb05E, 3GO8EulvT6g, 6ZSULlSKEuo, 6dBHDTA2tb4). 35bzZcacOcI holds both: Christian interpretation plus a report that religion means nothing to God. 4ZPHunKGo3U rejects worship outright. Islamic angelic imagery appears in 5f2y0Z5Bg7E; Muhammad appears in 6oA1G4FzJA4.
- **Heaven as place versus heaven as state.** A populated city with condos, palaces, and working angels in 6vIMiHpBpdk; an energetic state rather than a place in 72KqhyiRoWQ.
- **Tunnel and light versus no tunnel, no light, no visuals.** Tunnels in 1MXb5CkwyCk, 3WLVfgIMHkg, 3j7YqTZkRuE; explicitly no tunnel in 1WaxVu7benw and 3XurduW72c0; darkness and voices without light in 3erzR-tEY1Y; loving darkness in 77ubKhxGyQY; no OBE sensation despite paradise in 0sl-zBzqCRQ; no visuals in 7X3ydhGNhDI.
- **Transformation as gain versus transformation as cost.** Most cards report growth, but 1e4HSPc49FA reports PTSD and disability, 3yu0alwRjig two decades of depression, 3yiyBQuGUTE finds Earth horrible on return, 5oXU3jlImng retains anxiety needing therapy, and 1pzxPkYsT9A, 20pOtHi_FGg, 26BSMFkOrQE, and 5zN52hVfIR4 report little change.
- **Relatives as welcomers versus relatives as refusers.** Welcoming relatives in 0ISQ4ejRkXU and 08WPrvAAHdk; a woman who refuses assistance in 0DZ6THcU0Tk; relatives who say "You can't keep holding on to us" in 11DgYOavHlM.
- **Personal testimony versus mediated material.** Research talks, compilations, and non-NDE items inside the NDE category: -gKY-EmmTSQ, -PNYA4cso1g, -Grpll8tXbU, 3VMvm9I6NLk, 6pzoLuT1FmU, 7-sS-HPaJts, and 7Zsp5nvsMpw (transcript contains only "Hi").
