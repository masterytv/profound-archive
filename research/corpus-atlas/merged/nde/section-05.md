## Themes

Counts are sums of per-batch estimates. Motifs no batch counted as a theme, but which recur across standout and rare lists, are listed last with the number of batches in which they surface.

- **Peace, love, light and heightened reality:** approx. 577 of ~800. The dominant affect in every batch, usually described as more real than ordinary life and often tied to a bright light or being of light. Examples: Vn3nYBpXJ8w, W907DTdgUtE, Ws17qna56Ns, XldxulLPD5s, acs9b6ZIz_M.

## Tensions and contradictions

- **Benevolent afterlife versus punitive worlds.** Peace, love and forgiveness dominate (W-CozVLh-oU, W01t1uDY73c, XldxulLPD5s, YSa3El8VFOo, arDUy5S4YRI, cB5dQVyjAvg), but a persistent minority reports hell, demons, courtrooms, voids or accusation (W-y1sD7Kjt4, Vrnilxu3Wq8, WGJGlHTD1i8, WJWmngiT2Dg, XAGqQtj-MA4, XGsCcEWCAU8, XZ_AXQIom3Y, XqtLcCDHSO8, Y_jWqmGFU44, Yqmv2ntrpF4, YtaS1Zyrxnw, Z3iKLbXI1_A, _oPWOoPsjCI, _pOcUgjU-Sc, _v2qvMmp6WE, abu-HKJKQBk, amZKVHDf8Kc, b8METTvwzCQ, baquIFSE2h0, bxzM0utqwlc, c9dGzKgoly0, cBMqtLOSv1o). Many of the distressing journeys resolve into love (WTDPNUwRVXE, XTo_KXXQRdM, aguTPw_8goU, bllBdgGJmik).
- **Return as choice versus compulsion.** Chosen or negotiated return: Vz1NcqZkDTU, VwjP938ypq0, W8IMGtuSvUg, WHxnUEacbbE, Wclsh6SX7Dw, Ws17qna56Ns, X1SEgbDqLxE, XBwuCjgP64E, Xd5-oL3al-c, Y86ici7rNiE, Y9xliZBvtQQ, ZITs5lsdYNo, ZiyBlm2SV8Q, _6VRn0gmI20, _dKT9tKpX0E, a9ickOKUWaY, aSNVGewTc0M, acmhxiApSBs, bADe2AAO3QE, bcpvG_H3t0o, cK-1RBpZ3YY.
