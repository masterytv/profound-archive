## Tensions and contradictions

- Peaceful, loving destination versus hellish ordeal. Welcome and love dominate (t3DYRbVa9A0, u8Xsic0h0EE, vR2HFFE1rPA, wGw_el4rtp4, zBXlp8VPy6s), while a minority describe deceptive lizard-like entities, torment, forced labor, demons, or a barren planet (sjvD1FvIhdo, tIwCe4Ukync, turt5McjUlw, u3cJK0gx9KU, vHSc89_I4uY, w3JYT2ZWDOE, wJRWt952nk0, xvwvK3aA8OI, zHHbZTyIr_c). Hell language can also frame a benevolent journey: v4w1m5PqwZM promises "ALL of Hell" but delivers Jesus and Buddha teaching children.
- Chosen return versus forced return. Free choice is explicit in uag13tK1_LY, u-2Kukqqqko, vLxtAxMlM-c, x-HmHf2Vf2U, yFz2uKf5h70, and zp9uLaBP-wc; command, force, or bodily pull appears in uTDGvmlcS0g, uj0KLEEr30E, xMRJFryzJKg, xUwxhAxWHKE, yD3rxxn9HO0, zMCtMSFJsh8, and zlBYQaQR3_0. Some do not want to return at all (sS68CbEogTs, slZvbZJvHJU, srQxFzbiYuQ) or are upset at being sent back (t34rawQtKp4, szL7x2Jzk3k); zecVmC3Mcg8 has another version of the self decide.
- Christian specificity versus universalism or other traditions. Jesus and God structure stcYIqiQvjs, tgtEjmgA_Yc, xQCSh0wcOGo, zGbjkSmDvZk, and znGE_SJ8Dms; uVfuWa4V9T4 says heaven has no religion, z3aJ6drHpEM says all religions contain truth, z9Om3ejFazQ calls the Bible humanly altered, and vfhE_mK7inM uses an NDE to reverse Islamic theology. Jesus is radiant in tPul5D1RSrU but appears in ordinary human form with Buddha and Mary in tcbI5WuwAas, and is depicted as alienated from his own role in xWGjvGcuEcw.
- Evidence versus vividness. Greyson and Veridical scores diverge: uGdVYqvd0q0 has Greyson 30 and Veridical 9 while uGMtd3YN3B0 has Greyson 30 and Veridical 24; zOznihuSMP8 and zQTgiXdFZrw score 30/32 with Veridical 11 while zXB_F8EfDNA and zg3HnkSg38s score 23 and 24. tmT13Uuump0 and tjOEB4oSTT4 carry high ratings while their card notes say specific verifiable perceptions are lacking. Elaborate accounts rated low include sSAn9l7xHpM, xOrFsyD59EI, wrfI3D0sEQg, and yXSnS2jMY4k.
- Prolonged clinical death versus remembered experience. w_WtY63NnoM reports 85 minutes of cardiac arrest and nothing remembered, while wrfI3D0sEQg claims 12 hours in a morgue, wy9LnsfxVvQ 11 hours in heaven, zDQ1SN3fS9M more than 45 minutes, and zFDzx6PSPf8 24 hours, all with elaborate content.
- Darkness as terror versus darkness as home. Darkness precedes demons in tdRdrfiacns, tfrhnXCtB0k, and turt5McjUlw, but is peaceful and entity-free in tdDJHdlYyqM, the most peaceful place ever visited in woY6gvju-JA, velvety and blissful in xQY_PkKhiTY, and populated by "keepers of the dark" in xEKiZ8uXogY.
- Life review as compassion versus judgment, present versus absent. Judgment-free relational reviews appear in u9O_LnT6eJU, xOrFsyD59EI, and yjKuA5JNpTQ; judgment or anguish appears in zHHbZTyIr_c, xb2eHbj8huU, and v-ICN2NYHtI; u3n1-xjaU80 shows souls changing color; wvTexJFlePg reports no life review despite a strong divine encounter.
- Transformation from 43 to zero. upmgXkXBKII and yXSnS2jMY4k score 43 and zDSKHHuC-go 42, while vG6tKuNx0jo, t9VIxf5oGk8, and xvwvK3aA8OI record none. Reintegration itself can bring pain, alienation, and depression (xTBNyMiegdM, xWGjvGcuEcw), institutional silence (utWljcn7bfw), or decades-long shifts in personality (wTf30cpSaO0).
- Relatives recognizable versus transformed or neutral. Recognizable family appears in wuX35UPauSw and x-b9ltoFOk8; a deceased child is a grown woman in syIfnCJX3_c, a dog is a fur-less sphere of awareness in wqlzxHDuNB8, a son is an ambient presence in xFEEb59SVX8, and a relative is neutral in xpBa6BkY4Nw.
- Crisis-triggered NDE versus adjacent experience. Meditation, breathwork, energy healing, guided practice, and grief produce NDE-like content without a medical crisis (spkZ9jAuets, uLyjsiAeQqk, uK9ChOaBlkQ, wV7EAnf3rBg, wh5DaS6zyww, xFEEb59SVX8, zwqIXOFCQTc); STEs (uQmI4XXAQdw, uezUR_fh4Bc, yaWiPiE5jQs), an OBE (ypQRu62s2mE), ADCs (unen39z4H0Y, vsTYh95i7qw, t7kh1aruMw8), a lecture (w1O_wsPOM1A), and cards with no transcript (srP1Da5Axgo, vvmABrII35c, w0H1epM0GX0) sit inside the NDE label.
