## Tensions and contradictions

- Peaceful, loving destination versus hellish ordeal. Love and homecoming dominate (kqeLkhVoAsk, lXK7Pi9uKmU, mQm22E2QC-c, npuP7AxBBMs, rDb1ozw61Bw), while a minority describe outer darkness, demons, lakes of fire, mud, or torment (khewGvzeD5g, kjd8moRa0Qs, lpZW9u8HK2A, nG2rKPe34YA, o17HXmDu294, pQ_gw_cMyA0, q2JjYYBogSY, ragcEDleVcU, rNrqa7CaZEE); qTqUg_3lg2c treats hell as an escapable state rather than a place.
- Chosen return versus forced return. Choice or negotiation appears in kSM8mMSk_WA, kvGD-e4hgCU, lCpBa1xgFv0, oZPv524iVko, riaULQA99iI, and s8ZOubpLuZE; forced or commanded return appears in kPvjjjYWNaE, ktixNw47KBQ (pulled "almost violently"), kknBKk9LZ9c ("Not yet" followed by a physical smack), lRD4w8HJ5gI, nGPmOvWo9QU, qSEBls7kvpo, rClF0LAEt5k, and sHikwn-JZ70. Some resent the return (lRD4w8HJ5gI, qplXvHsx33Y, s2AgMnJl4QA) or wish to go back to the dark realm (ry9ARwjCwD8).
- Family as anchor versus family forgotten. Children and spouses pull people back in l-JAfDhOyK0, lHmiH9F7kcs, neYfIZk8Pg8, and oY2avBe4cWo, yet l5kQTT7p19I reports guilt for not worrying about family and ocQWhCocVjo reports no concern for her son while approaching the light.
- Christian figures versus nonsectarian or plural cosmologies. Jesus, God, and angels center kTSAolN01cQ, kvyHQ3VQ73k, lZygVd31yNc, nFCh1bI0uHk, rUxPWg5II5Y, and sKGjt6TwEek; other accounts describe Source, oneness, impersonal consciousness, ETs, or mathematics (k_Iwa790eY0, kfmkyqG6oO4, lFUCS9nZijM, lgumAeBtwgo, lcNTSlq5Q7A, rc2WYBblvOw, sG6ddqOnavE). pvtuKyDV1WE has an entity refuse worship, and qt5G8JVIGns frames the afterlife as nonreligious.
- Life review as compassion versus life review as pain. Nonjudgmental or self-directed reviews appear in le1upcSMCN4, mx2QJp0oRs8, onghPOQEFAU, and s3s56iUENd4 (no mistakes reported), while n11e8yIKYxc, liyewCmXHmA, lPC5e_fePY0, q63925h8eaY, and rorJ2VvTrYQ describe painful confrontation or reliving harms from others' perspectives.
- High evidential ratings versus unverifiable vividness. Specific observations earn high ratings (kQ26a98telc, kmdazqHJUps, mL3nT0cAZ2g, mtbRakHU60M, nOY8fMwFrCc, opcQONTQHXQ, oyHX9Y5NmYs, sAjRH6nL5Gc), while equally vivid accounts explicitly lack verifiable perceptions (kRRgWV2lIvc, lAAT4ZU6PYU, leTKJVUjHR4, mr1wAaslf2k, px-S9hIDEyk, qAEUmq4uEtw, rw-oO4vDPxs). High Greyson scores do not track evidence: riuoB-pX4Ok has 30/32 with Veridical 10, ragcEDleVcU has 20/32 with Veridical 21.
- Transformation present versus absent. Scores of 36 to 43 appear in kZID6SvHrmk, neYfIZk8Pg8, oP1_6lK6UJY, and p5DLmCf6WaE, but kSOppBlVPnM, mI8YHUVR5Hw, pG24QSaSocg, q2JjYYBogSY, and s1Fke5LnbjA record no transformation, mjDciFjcR5U records Greyson 0 and Transformation 0, and oOo6YoZPNDw records fear of death increasing. p3aAk8AKRQg describes decades of isolation and alcohol problems before integration.
- Darkness as terror versus darkness as home. Frightening darkness appears in khewGvzeD5g and n3NZzNyHp3c, while komSA2YsqSo, qDaE0L85ZBQ, rVTwY7-7kNo, ry9ARwjCwD8, and rzejxrSj9Mk describe a warm, loving, or intelligent dark.
- Tunnel versus no tunnel, journey versus immediate arrival. Tunnels structure r27tdq6tbK4 and rA8Zua9Dw8s, while rHWBZSfWTVs goes directly to the light, rKMHsB3Z--8 explicitly has no tunnel, and pgkLqJX5q1g appears in heaven without a journey.
- Relatives as loving guides versus neutral or distant figures. The father in oYOKSioBjl4 is loving, the mother and brother in oj8LiGnBMZ4 are neutral, Thomas Jose says not to hug him in onghPOQEFAU, and r2f6TAE7neY presents deceased sisters as "short blue angels."
- One discrete episode versus many. nrv6j2162mc reports about eight exits over two days and rbB_bKCViAA reports twenty NDEs in one hospitalization, while nni0hJHd5Ls has a clinically dead episode without a clearly sequenced narrative.
- Pipeline classification versus content. kniiM3TGT48 (child welfare commentary), sG506zu4FW0 (a chameleon story), oQvnnQB3HMc (a book discussion), and qQS-mwizhD8 (no extracted experience) sit beside full 15-element cards such as kqeLkhVoAsk, lLH_m4DqoW4, oFAOr3qncpE, and s9PL3Bmodrg.
