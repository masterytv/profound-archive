## Themes

Counts are sums of per-batch estimates where a batch named the theme. Batches often folded related motifs together, so totals are approximate and overlapping.

1. **Peace, love, light, and acceptance.** approx. 585 of ~800. The dominant register in every batch: unconditional love, relief from pain, and a welcoming realm, often described as more real than ordinary life. Examples: 7hlBs6UP0kE, 82aema6PX7A, 9fPYQtRlHC8, DANVCZJrSwY, ESwAMF-wBsc.
2. **Return as assignment, family duty, or command.** approx. 570 of ~800. Experiencers are sent, pulled, or choose to return for children, unfinished work, or a message to deliver. Examples: 7hQqj2IUF-0, 8u94J_PNadM, ACneU_WZYjg, As9o57usxuI, EkQq7-3w0_A.
3. **Spiritual transformation, reduced fear of death, and service.** approx. 480 of ~800. Compassion, purpose, and new helping vocations recur. Examples: 7yTJlCHNb44, 9oIfs0RVqqw, B8415mYtxoM, DXQh28N7eis, FHLJmn0f46o.
4. **Deceased relatives, guides, angels, and named religious figures.** approx. 340 of ~800. Relatives as validators and return messengers; Jesus, God, angels, councils, and guides as instructors. Examples: 7qvswwOhcoI, 86cIgJbbnuw, 9a6ihWJhHfA, ASY1W2ftCBY, EhdvWLYssMk.
5. **Life review and moral empathy.** approx. 320 of ~800. Reviews built from other people's feelings, from felt harm, or from nonjudgmental learning. Examples: 7k3aZR0Fmo8, 9m77ID3wC6s, D3MlrVCbspk, EhJoiaSTp7Y, FJBZ5rEsJQM.
6. **Out-of-body observation.** approx. 305 of ~800 (counted in 11 of 20 batches). Watching resuscitation, surgery, or the accident scene from above, with sharply uneven evidential weight. Examples: 7nMe0T6TgNo, A6QE2uNPBXg, By3UjYUe7rw, DAUBS-83Lbo, EHwBXZXzByM.
7. **Cosmic unity, expanded identity, and elaborate cosmologies.** approx. 50 of ~800 counted separately (batches 022, 029, 039), otherwise folded into love and peace. ## Tensions and contradictions

- **Positive welcome versus hellish realms.** Peace and love dominate (82aema6PX7A, 8Sq1CKnfaKs, 9NXyfhw-lb0, CEo_b-a8XUA, FHYnljT3lAU), while a persistent minority begin in demons, dungeons, or ruined cities before rescue (7fvZqsRyFQ8, 7nmCYK0L1BU, 83Nvsf80Cv4, 8YGDBN9X7R8, 94W8gN0hKmI, 9bPQh_ZBzVw, 9kMKbzZACSY, 9s9mn9OCyN0, A3V1ccODlTM, A2NGV591_Tw, BHggP2VgfS4, BcU5CVXJVvY, BlqIHQOfM3U, C1i66xR5B2Q, DB4LIhewdGk, DZuKenphL8U, E4qZlc17IyY, EtoxEQB-3JI, FYZlD9MUMis). CCO1IvDz1xE and AE_8E6casn0 report fear that lasts.
- **Hell is real versus hell is not real.** Hell as a consequential realm in 94W8gN0hKmI, 9HetcJ2geiM, 98VNA_ja3sM, and BlqIHQOfM3U; "Hell's not real" told to Josas in 8r4SsV8apCw; God says sin and judgment do not exist in Bzh2puwUODw; 7lZFeSlUlLo explicitly did not see hell; A-kHECRK9pQ says suffering does not affect the soul; 8YGDBN9X7R8 frames hell as a place people "Found a Way Out" of.
- **Choice versus compulsion, and outright refusal.** Free choice: 7hlBs6UP0kE, 82aema6PX7A, 9lN-gwjKSUM, EODDzooJ6Nw ("I agreed of my own free will"), EZWke6kWbjU. Commanded or forced: 7nPQVPbxJD4, 8EWPOWLA4ro, 9miac9B9XCo, 9z_axo_GaVo (zapped), AEgxTodIs3o, ASY1W2ftCBY, Blp5Q9x0L5c, D3Ql06RhYlI, EzPYMmFV5Bo. - **Life review as empathy versus accusation versus absence.** Nonjudgmental learning in 9m77ID3wC6s, A9V7H6BBBkw, AMuNQl3Sxa0, DkETL0d-Wa4, FJBZ5rEsJQM; self-judgment or accusation in A5s0IoWf7Rs, AFNoPeKNm4g; punitive empathy in EhJoiaSTp7Y; tests and temptations in E3F2a0r2F9w; no review at all in 7nPQVPbxJD4; reality as something manifested rather than reviewed in 7lqoqg19odA.
