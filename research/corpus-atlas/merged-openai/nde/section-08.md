## Themes

- Love, peace, light, and homecoming, approx. 505 of 703. The dominant atmosphere is unconditional love, safety, radiance, unity, or relief from bodily pain, sometimes expressed as darkness rather than light. Examples: sSI2u9hYCgQ, stIIt5si94U, vR2HFFE1rPA, xOrFsyD59EI, zqNvAKnJiMw.
- Return as choice, command, duty, or mission, approx. 455 of 703 from reported batch counts. Return is linked to children, family, unfinished work, service, pre-birth agreements, or a direct instruction that the experiencer's time has not come. Examples: sRvOATE-84M, u-2Kukqqqko, vLxtAxMlC, xUwxhAxWHKE, z-pgxwtxZE0.
- Spiritual transformation and reduced fear of death, approx. 477 of 703. Accounts commonly report increased compassion, changed priorities, spiritual certainty, service, healing work, or an altered relationship to death. Examples: sbz_B4AHYVU, upmgXkXBKII, whDi9xOppRQ, yXSnS2jMY4k, zDSKHHuC-go.
- Out-of-body observation and altered embodiment, approx. 390 of 703 from documented counts. Experiencers observe bodies, medical teams, rescue scenes, or accident sites, or describe consciousness as separate from the body. Examples: sSzHyg8V3oo, t1QdUV4khoE, vYIOAUtA3Oo, yuoZ6bPnSKk, zoq0jfCN_8Q.
- Life review and moral or empathic learning, approx. 260 of 703 from documented counts. Reviews may involve panoramic images, future paths, the emotional effects of actions on others, forgiveness, or moral judgment. Examples: sfObAQI6Kcw, tBctJJH_ZUY, xOrFsyD59EI, yFXkrxYP3_c, zDQ1SN3fS9M.
- Religious figures, guides, and culturally specific interpreters, approx. 200 of 703 across reported counts. Jesus, God, angels, Mary, Yeshua, Gabriel, Shiva, Buddha, Archangel Michael, and unnamed guides provide comfort, instruction, rescue, or judgment. Examples: seR6Wa0qfbw, tZgb_vg0Q1U, uTDGvmlcS0g, xxi8LRwiGjs, znGE_SJ8Dms.
- Deceased relatives, pets, and relational continuity, approx. 170 of 703 across reported counts. Family members, spouses, children, grandparents, dogs, and other animals appear as companions, validators, rescuers, or reasons to return. Examples: sZqx9B2RcfA, t24kqOtVSKI, uloRuCk6UFg, wqlzxHDuNB8, z1QtKaNnN8o.
- Cosmic unity, expanded identity, and universal consciousness, approx. 150 of 703 from reported counts. The self is described as source, awareness, a fragment of a whole, or the cosmos itself, often with knowledge downloads and non-linear time. - Light, tunnel, darkness, and nonordinary geography, approx. 140 of 703 from reported counts. Experiences include tunnels, crystal cities, rivers, libraries, planetary settings, peaceful darkness, and realms described as heaven or hell. Examples: sh1jUjgSI_8, u8qFFFd2AmQ, wrfI3D0sEQg, woY6gvju-JA, zFDzx6PSPf8.

Examples: spkZ9jAuets, uGMtd3YN3B0, vxnmDhs6Nrg, yFXkrxYP3_c, zg3HnkSg38s.
- Childhood NDEs and child-linked cosmologies, approx. 50 of 703 from reported counts. Children report guides, unity, animal perspectives, future identities, spiritual playgrounds, and messages for parents. Examples: tP8HUfnpSDA, tXGvgc-XfbQ, vFINxFxEbQs, yh1EMNV1L7U, yHw6RZYC004.
- Hell, demons, judgment, and distressing routes, approx. 50 of 703 from reported counts. Examples: sjvD1FvIhdo, tIwCe4Ukync, wJRWt952nk0, xvwvK3aA8OI, zHHbZTyIr_c.
- Prophecy, future visions, and messages about humanity, approx. 50 of 703 from reported counts. Future spouses, wars, political upheaval, disasters, alternate timelines, and claims about humanity's transformation appear alongside return missions. Examples: sfObAQI6Kcw, tgP4FGKLpgA, xrLci4N_UyE, zOqcnAFu-7o, zzL08IiI06E.
- Reincarnation, pre-birth planning, and soul contracts, approx. 35 of 703 from reported examples. Accounts describe choosing parents, multiple lives, simultaneous incarnations, life contracts, soul families, and planned trauma or missions. Examples: tBHnLcs82DE, u3WqNlnPNCU, xMqfErvWHo8, zGGTJ-dj0OY, zs6hUE2npaQ.

Afterlife settings are presented as educational systems containing lives, futures, planets, moral information, or universal laws. Examples: sblDhhqP6TQ, u-_bkoxO0uM, uGMtd3YN3B0, xQgpVnW4V5w, zDSKHHuC-go.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Love-centered destinations dominate across the group, but sustained hell narratives recur in sjvD1FvIhdo, tIwCe4Ukync, turt5McjUlw, wJRWt952nk0, xvwvK3aA8OI, and zHHbZTyIr_c. Other accounts place hell imagery alongside rescue, forgiveness, or a positive life review, including tZgb_vg0Q1U, v4w1m5PqwZM, yDvYq4BhY38, and zTsrlEfrTXU.
- Return is remembered as free choice in uag13tK1_LY, v9wqDJi6tFs, x-HmHf2Vf2U, and zp9uLaBP-wc, but as command, coercion, or physical compulsion in uj0KLEEr30E, vBycNsnhc7Q, xUwxhAxWHKE, and zDSKHHuC-go. This tension recurs in nearly every batch.
- Family obligation can appear as love, duty, or constraint. Survivors return for children in tIkz_plTSbU, w539EzriIT0, whDi9xOppRQ, z1QtKaNnN8o, and zg3HnkSg38s, while other experiencers resist leaving the afterlife in t24kqOtVSKI, vLxtAxMlC, and xUwxhAxWHKE.
- Religious specificity conflicts with universalist or nondual accounts. Jesus, God, Mary, and Christian rescue are central in sh1jUjgSI_8, tZgb_vg0U1, znGE_SJ8Dms, and zyltZXUXEDE, while formless unity, source, or nonreligious consciousness appears in t3DYRbVa9A0, uVfuWa4V9T4, xMqfErvWHo8, and yiBDUB4CX2I.
- High Greyson or intensity scores do not reliably predict evidential strength. uGdVYqvd0q0 and zOznihuSMP8 have Greyson 30 with lower Veridical ratings, while uGMtd3YN3B0, vxnmDhs6Nrg, and zg3HnkSg38s have stronger evidential ratings. Vivid spiritual narratives can explicitly lack veridical claims, as in swem7MixxP8, vHRwItzxnnE, and z3Q38QgDRrg.
- Life review is often framed as empathy rather than punishment in sfObAQI6Kcw, xOrFsyD59EI, xb2eHbj8huU, and zDQ1SN3fS9M, but as judgment, torment, or repentance in tIwCe4Ukync, w3JYT2ZWDOE, zHHbZTyIr_c, and zTsrlEfrTXU.
- The group contains elaborate experiences and apparent non-experiences. w_WtY63NnoM reports 85 minutes of clinical death but no remembered NDE, while rich journeys appear in sblDhhqP6TQ, xQgpVnW4V5w, and v6Cqy64E4Ho.
- The archive boundary is unstable. Conventional medical NDEs sit beside STEs, OBEs, ADCs, shared-death experiences, meditation, and guided practice in uQmI4XXAQdw, unen39z4H0Y, v-jqDHMQp_s, yaWiPiE5jQs, and zwqIXOFCQTc.

## Book-angle sparks

- **Who chooses the return?** Compare free choice, family ethics, assigned mission, rescue, bodily compulsion, and return through another self: uag13tK1_LY, w539EzriIT0, xUwxhAxWHKE, zecVmC3Mcg8.
- **The life review as moral technology:** Set empathic reliving beside judgment and hell, asking whether the review teaches, punishes, or repairs relationships: sfObAQI6Kcw, xOrFsyD59EI, zDQ1SN3fS9M, zHHbZTyIr_c.
- **One light, many theologies:** Compare Jesus, God, Mary, Buddha, Gabriel, nondual source, and culturally specific guides without reducing them to one vocabulary: tZgb_vg0U1, v4w1m5PqwZM, xxi8LRwiGjs, yiBDUB4CX2I, zwNu1kiaNe8.
- **The evidential NDE:** Examine why hospital observations and unusual sensory claims receive high ratings while equally elaborate cosmologies receive low ratings: uGMtd3YN3B0, vxnmDhs6Nrg, zg3HnkSg38s, xOrFsyD59EI.
- **Darkness as safety:** Reframe dark or minimally populated experiences against the assumption that darkness means fear: woY6gvju-JA, xQY_PkKhiTY, xALuIaS-K40.
- **Children as cosmological witnesses:** Ask whether childhood accounts are unusually direct, culturally shaped, or especially difficult to corroborate: tP8HUfnpSDA, tXGvgc-XfbQ, yh1EMNV1L7U, yHw6RZYC004.
- **Animals as afterlife persons:** Trace pets and animal guides as companions, communicators, rescuers, and moral subjects rather than symbols: uVfuWa4V9T4, wqlzxHDuNB8, wGw_el4rtp4, z0gSjrRF8nk.
- **Afterlife as archive or classroom:** Compare libraries, Book of Life imagery, Akashic Records, life databases, and structured knowledge downloads: sblDhhqP6TQ, uGMtd3YN3B0, u-_bkoxO0uM, xQgpVnW4V5w.
- **From experiencer to investigator:** Explore how NDEs become missions of healing, research, public testimony, or service: sSAn9l7xHpM, yHw6RZYC004, yVxCeJVXGi8, zDSKHHuC-go.
- **Reintegration as the neglected half of the NDE:** Focus on pain, alienation, guilt, altered identity, changed priorities, and difficulty returning after the experience: xTBNyMiegdM, xWGjvGcuEcw, wGaSPZ1gVpk, yiBDUB4CX2I.
- **Prophecy and the temptation of cosmic scale:** Contrast future visions about wars, disasters, political change, and humanity with the archive's evidential uncertainty: tgP4FGKLpgA, xrLci4N_UyE, zOqcnAFu-7o, zzL08IiI06E.
- **Beyond the conventional NDE:** Define what changes when ADCs, STEs, shared death, meditation, lucid practice, and rescue visions enter the same archive: unen39z4H0Y, v-jqDHMQp_s, yaWiPiE5jQs, zwqIXOFCQTc.
