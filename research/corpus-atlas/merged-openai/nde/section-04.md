## Themes

- **Love, peace, light, and unity, approx. 590 of 801:** The dominant affect is overwhelming peace, unconditional love, homecoming, oneness, or a more-real-than-life light. (NfMBKf_WG_I, O5m1Z5hEnH4, Oediqi3PkUk, PpY5ltV78gQ, QkEJCM6GId0, RlAlRefLdAY, S6v6IjBZofk, U_ifO8QrUXU, V6DKsqTs9M8, VOBEOvAgm4I)
- **Return as choice, mission, family duty, or command, approx. 552 of 801:** Experiencers return for children, partners, service, unfinished work, or because a guide, relative, or authority sends them back. (NoQxPQ1Kjgc, OBxPn0Y3nl0, PK7PTC3Qw4s, Qg29aKfNLIY, RZm22oA4o9A, S9NhDYQIRfQ, T5giGHYv4kU, U631WWYotds, UYfEmggQK4E, VcnpKOC3KjI)
- **Spiritual transformation and reduced fear of death, approx. 520 of 801:** Common aftermaths include compassion, service, changed priorities, spiritual awareness, gratitude, and reduced fear of dying. (OLGWAewZDp0, OMr1ntfvwgg, Oediqi3PkUk, PEVfquGhBhg, Psi1qYOf2fs, QN6UdytCcFg, RHo-5-KHl4I, S1E02DuC3R4, UPTgLm8AVyo, Vml9nh382RI)
- **Out-of-body observation and separation from the body, approx. 400 of 801:** Accounts repeatedly describe watching the body, medical staff, rescuers, accidents, or the surrounding scene from above or elsewhere. (NgxoLHd4XcM, O4iKKsqXbsY, Ohr6ut_5AJw, QAsOPvGTB1c, RuiRkvga5pU, UEnE61R_aLI, UYBGm_rDvso, V65jl0VCE4g, VbvV4etSRoY)
- **Life review as empathy and moral consequence, approx. 330 of 801:** Reviews often involve reliving effects on other people, feeling others' pain, self-judgment, compassion, or learning rather than simple courtroom punishment. (NhTkmpI6Lfw, OIo2kFjcWHs, OX-Ak86BHPw, Q7JfC0U0_Fc, Q0ssO8KvmNA, RCQkIutaqgs, RVuU5U_0beI, S4yUVx37V7I, T1ZdD7KEZXk, UJyHra52zgA)
- **Deceased relatives, friends, pets, and soul families, approx. 330 of 801:** Familiar dead commonly provide reassurance, information, guidance, or a reason to return, with occasional deceased animals and soul-family frameworks. (Nk48t-FQW_Q, OhoidSzUaxk, PDWsegFosM0, QDEv0ul8QiI, Qu1YnoEkfHs, R4YI-7rq01Y, SFX9cKYFEtQ, T-_nrre93nQ, U64hTJxoTlM, VYf0s6KuBho)
- **Religious figures alongside nonsectarian or pluralistic cosmology, approx. 300 of 801:** Jesus, God, angels, Buddha, Shiva, Vishnu, Mother Mary, guides, energy, intelligence, and oneness appear in overlapping but nonidentical vocabularies. (O3Ff6Hc4O7Y, OBxPn0Y3nl0, OizX472ycCw, QJepDqYO5iw, S9PQHOeqzmw, T6fEH_hoLh0, U8Ae4PMAAqk, VOBEOvAgm4I)
- **Expanded consciousness, telepathy, and direct knowing, approx. 220 of 801:** Knowledge is often described as instantaneous, telepathic, or too extensive to retain, including cosmic history, future events, and identity beyond the body. (P9OeOMHzuK4, PmPI_xqtwZc, QgFtJYYOHEU, Qs1yUk-C8Qo, S14VrsILt6U, Tebz0cidZds, U5ULZ5GUZJY, V0uwO7rt8-0)
- **Tunnels, boundaries, light passage, and structured realms, approx. 210 of 801:** Many narratives use tunnels, gates, bridges, walls, cities, gardens, libraries, orientation centers, or other transitional architectures, though some report no movement or visuals. (NoQxPQ1Kjgc, PBrBoc2C0Sc, Qd7mxjPtonE, SE_YP7uziVs, S4kBlnrPHAA, UYBGm_rDvso, VIlM_fGTI9M)
- **Childhood and repeated NDEs, approx. 150 of 801:** Children report unusually early cosmologies, telepathy, future knowledge, or pre-birth purpose, while adults sometimes describe multiple episodes across years or a lifetime. (NhTkmpI6Lfw, NswSgqFkVk4, Ojh4PQV4_MY, QN6UdytCcFg, Rq08yncYknI, S4kBlnrPHAA, UYde3J7uaEY, U_ifO8QrUXU)
- **Distressing, hellish, demonic, or void experiences, approx. 110 of 801:** A minority describe hell, darkness, demons, torment, threatening beings, judgment, or featureless emptiness, sometimes followed by rescue, love, or transformation. (NqqoCcsQWQo, OQ4tqcGsK-4, OY2aK2A-w7s, OupepTZCUZ0, RNooA4tlBmU, S7T5qmE6jsU, UItXrmtyE28, Vhi6wFU46ec)
- **Future visions, prophecy, and alternate timelines, approx. 100 of 801:** Some accounts claim foreknowledge of disasters, wars, political events, future lives, planetary changes, or conditional possibilities. (Ogy7BKnMFiA, Ot_oAFw1Qfw, PXcwud8kj8A, Rq08yncYknI, StgIx5jdIxA, ThvrdY4SDEI, UPJT57ost_E, V6XXFICMXLU)
- **Pre-birth planning, reincarnation, and selected hardship, approx. 85 of 801:** Several accounts frame incarnation as planned, with selected parents, bodies, injuries, assignments, difficulties, past lives, or possible future lives. (P7D7XaXiNVc, PSbQJJr8A18, PtTl_qJ_3zE, TbWccmzIMa0, TfLLY_HdWT8, U06cUVPepqA, V1ipsOOZ_Gk, VYf0s6KuBho)
- **Veridical perception and evidential claims, approx. 80 of 801:** High archive Veridical ratings cluster around operating-room observations, overheard conversations, remote viewing, predicted locations, and messages later treated as externally checkable. (Ng_Z0HfG_UE, O0A0Fr1-AN8, OQdS9iDDJzk, Oediqi3PkUk, Ogy7BKnMFiA, RgQmKWNP3DE, T6fEH_hoLh0, UEnE61R_aLI, VbvV4etSRoY)
- **Shared death experiences and after-death communication, approx. 30 of 801:** A small cluster involves witnessing another person's transition, accompanying a deceased loved one, receiving later messages, or sharing an experience with family. (NwxLC_Rjgrc, NyC1unA5mIk, QOfompyimoE, RSNZ71JZc1E, Shutbt-7NdQ, TSVj0swJRCA, VGU8gL5JwMA, VJgjQMW2C_o)
- **Nonstandard OBEs, STEs, UAPs, and anomalous aftermaths, approx. 25 of 801:** The archive includes waking ADCs, unusual OBEs, later UAP sightings, electronic anomalies, hypnosis, and experiences that the cards do not clearly classify as NDEs. (PlRS0k4rnpA, PVeP9pwhD8E, QjSxTEAu22U, RFFuXrJxmro, RxMvSA-P0lE, U0-6LEiJd7c, V_R9ChlTfCE)

## Tensions and contradictions

- **Love and welcome versus hell, demons, and torment:** The dominant accounts describe peace, safety, and unconditional love, including NfMBKf_WG_I, PpY5ltV78gQ, and S6v6IjBZofk. In contrast, NqqoCcsQWQo, OQ4tqcGsK-4, S7T5qmE6jsU, UItXrmtyE28, and Vhi6wFU46ec describe hellish realms, demons, darkness, or terror.
- **Chosen return versus forced return:** Some experiencers explicitly choose or request return, including Ot5byhyBYHM, PwKRiyk4PNQ, Qg29aKfNLIY, and VD0Pd7twFBY. Others report commands, compulsion, or inability to remain, including NmkDNVg8pdg, OXUgsj1Dbho, RzEYqGzDSfU, T5giGHYv4kU, and UFLuryTjOoo.
- **Family obligation versus personal mission:** Return is sometimes anchored in children or a partner, as in NoQxPQ1Kjgc, RSNZ71JZc1E, RZm22oA4o9A, and VJgjQMW2C_o. Elsewhere it is framed as service, testimony, healing, or unfinished work, as in OBxPn0Y3nl0, OhoidSzUaxk, and U631WWYotds.
- **Tunnel and movement versus timeless unity or void:** NoQxPQ1Kjgc, Qd7mxjPtonE, and VIlM_fGTI9M report tunnels or passage, while Rex Finfgeld in Ns4NQiM1Stk, QiW4rgle2Bk, and VDm14xmdLd8 describe instantaneous unity, limbo, or no light and no conventional travel.
- **Religious specificity versus pluralism and loss of faith:** Jesus, God, angels, and Christian salvation appear in O3Ff6Hc4O7Y, QHNW29X-nrE, and SnFdfEvy6L4. Other accounts invoke Buddha, Shiva, Vishnu, energy, or nondual consciousness, including OizX472ycCw, T6fEH_hoLh0, U8Ae4PMAAqk, and V6DKsqTs9M8. VOBEOvAgm4I explicitly complicates the expectation that transcendence produces conventional faith.
- **Narrative intensity versus evidential strength:** Rich, element-dense accounts can have low or moderate Veridical ratings, including Ot5byhyBYHM, RJeL8me8zFM, U5ULZ5GUZJY, and VOBEOvAgm4I. Conversely, sparse accounts receive high ratings, including RgQmKWNP3DE and T6fEH_hoLh0.
- **Transformation versus delayed or minimal aftereffects:** Many accounts report major change, such as RVuU5U_0beI, PEVfquGhBhg, and UfYstzc8E3I. Others report little immediate effect or minimal documented transformation, including PqddJsxn16s, P95V4rsE1HE, Rx0gK67fqCo, and S-7XkWCWwas.
- **Clear NDE sequence versus ambiguous source material:** Conventional accounts with high element counts sit beside OBEs, STEs, ADCs, historical summaries, incomplete transcripts, and cards with no clear NDE sequence, including PUW3DBLAJJk, PVeP9pwhD8E, QMKHmPkHH-0, RxMvSA-P0lE, S7-LOH8hVTE, and TOvp2vvxjH8.
