- Witnessed aerial objects and formal investigation, about 14 of 40. Several accounts involve pilots, military personnel, multiple witnesses, authorities or media follow-up rather than isolated observation. Examples: H7t9j3XDHuw, HNSJIV-jx8M.

- H8ia9CtRWLg, "Eyewitnesses talk about observing debris from the 1947 crashed alien craft in Roswell". It illustrates the retrieval narrative and the tension between physical testimony and institutional control.
- HEAcbpuDFB8, "Key Takeaways from Jake Barber’s Incredible UFO Claims". A former F-16 pilot and alleged crash-retrieval participant describes a nonhuman craft, a spiritual connection and later health problems attributed to radiation. It combines secrecy, claimed government programs and transformative contact.
- HNSJIV-jx8M, "Huge UFO in the skies over Paris witnessed by Air France Captain Jean-Charles Duboc & crew, 1994". A large disc was observed by an airline crew in clear conditions, reported to air traffic control and studied by French authorities. It stands out for strong observational detail and an unresolved official investigation.
- HNpXUWzVygo, "Terry Lovelace 2019 - Incident at Devils Den". Lovelace describes a triangular craft, beams, small beings and later intrusive memories, with high evidence, contact and transformation ratings. It illustrates the classic occupant encounter merged with delayed psychological impact.

- HOb1O9zU8wk, "Citizen journalists find three UAP in New York". Citizen investigators used infrared equipment, reported objects to the FBI and described a luminous object moving against the wind. It illustrates contemporary instrument-assisted witnessing and grassroots investigation.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Retrieval and nonhuman interpretations are asserted in Roswell debris, naval hardware and Barber's account, while Laxson explicitly believed the craft was American technology and one survey video rejects alien explanations for ancient sites. The opposing sources are H8ia9CtRWLg, H9GSqOEvoBE, HEAcbpuDFB, HLfdFG31XUg and HExGOTZ9T88.
- Some accounts frame contact as telepathic, spiritual or consciousness-mediated, while other well-documented sightings report no contact at all. Compare HCFU7Lc4xvI, HHtKB6aVhB8 and HBYPKjM3ftw with HNSJIV-jx8M and HFUKr0s5xfs.
- The batch alternates between calls for transparency and descriptions of secrecy, ridicule, detention or manipulated memory. Compare HNApQNqzCC8 and HB5e4mgJX2Q with H8ia9CtRWLg and HKitoV3VLts.
- Transformation is uneven: Davids and Kaycee report major worldview change, whereas Laxson reports no lasting impact and the Paris sighting is rated transformation zero. Sources: HD2gtX140bM, HNKi8KcyaLM, HLfdFG31XUg, HNSJIV-jx8M.
