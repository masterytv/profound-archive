- **Transformation is uneven:** Some witnesses report major worldview or life changes, including uJsiXa4tEEw and uDwKoGVbasY, while the cards explicitly report no lasting impact for the witnesses in u8vYE9PpPvc and no lasting impact beyond initial shock for Renzo in uHoqGEb21EU.
- **Contact ranges from physical and entity-based to purely observational:** Mario Seuka saw two humanoid entities and experienced physical effects in uCPDKe240OM, while Eric Delgado's high-altitude sighting involved a silent, vanishing light with no described occupants in u9eSLBxJ7yU.
- **Evidence is framed both scientifically and speculatively:** uHr3SzEtNdM calls for advanced methods, observatories and major funding, whereas uWHy9xFQWl0 presents Mu claims that the card says historians and scientists reject for lack of evidence.
- **Disclosure is portrayed as possible institutional release and deliberate manipulation:** uEb4S4UcUBY advocates stronger disclosure and whistleblower protections, while uUvmYKZXIGo warns that alien narratives could be used as propaganda to create fear.
