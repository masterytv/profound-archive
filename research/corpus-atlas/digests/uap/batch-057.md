## Tensions and contradictions

- Some accounts frame disclosure as an active government or media deception, while others present disclosure as a document-driven public process. The deception framing appears in II68XTOuozQ and IMylY_8k02c, while IJEWRG4Y6-I focuses on task-force slides, redactions and release.
- Encounters are variously treated as physically evidential or primarily psychological and psi-related. IHWXhL6g9lU reports landing marks and footprints, and INMDU03ESok reports soil testing, while I6TQPUn8dUE and IIuRssjXVvA emphasize consciousness, telepathy and visions.
- Contact is not consistently traumatic. IAEsS4IOrBk describes terror and PTSD, whereas I6TQPUn8dUE describes a joyful connection and IQXUTnmxTtA describes movement from fear to acceptance.
- High contact does not imply moral certainty. IPD_4AE_5QI explicitly states that high-level contact does not guarantee moral integrity, while several accounts present entities as reassuring or loving, including IIuN5CiLXLM and I6TQPUn8dUE.
