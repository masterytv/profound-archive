- Some narratives describe encounters as frightening, destabilizing or psychologically damaging [9k7KlYj1a80], [9ytuFTfK9Ls], while CE5 and lifelong-contact accounts describe growth, enlightenment or positive development [9nwX7OiyGF4], [9rSmfsKP1Q8].
