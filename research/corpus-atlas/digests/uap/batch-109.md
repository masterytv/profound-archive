Neither card establishes a nonhuman origin.
- **Nonhuman craft versus secret human technology:** Several accounts attribute advanced objects to extraterrestrial or nonhuman intelligence (ZlRkF8Q-yMc, Zrd44LASEKI), while the black-technology documentary explicitly keeps advanced military projects as an alternative and remains skeptical of extraterrestrial explanations (Zzk7XpAfHiI).
- **Contact as threatening versus cooperative:** The Idaho encounter includes vehicle control by an alien being (Zf3J3-tyZTc), whereas James describes deliberate, meditation-assisted contact and a desire to educate others (ZupKv8lTLWU).
- **Disclosure as confirmation versus qualification:** David Grusch's testimony is summarized as confirming serious claims about alien craft and reverse engineering (Zrd44LASEKI), while Edgar Mitchell confirms Roswell as real but provides no specific details about alien involvement (Zu_7hd5B-dc).
