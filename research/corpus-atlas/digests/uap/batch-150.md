## Standout accounts

- **m_eoMSI1KNU, Extraordinary 1976 UFO incident in Tehran, Iran: Mother Ship sighting, object chased by fighter jets.** A military encounter combines multiple witnesses, radar or instrumentation problems, attempted weapons engagement and an official investigation. It illustrates physical effects, military testimony and disclosure pressure.
- **mjo-RqASuB0, Rare interviews and articles with children who witnessed the Ariel school UFO landing in Ruwa, 1994.** The account links a large group sighting and telepathic communication with ecological activism, continuing dreams and one child experiencing psychological difficulty. It illustrates psi content and divergent transformation.
- **mR06kvrbtZg, This Alien Abduction Followed One Family for Generations!** The account presents a multigenerational family narrative, paralysis, hypnosis, alleged child abduction and long-term trauma. It illustrates the intimate, inherited form of the abduction theme.
- **mlCP7hiHPQk, The Pattern Revealed (S1, E3).** Sean Cahill's account connects a military witness, a persistent unresolved observation, institutional ridicule and later investigation. It is a strong example of stigma followed by validation.
- **mlUo-Vn6T2g, 2 huge, silent black triangles over Monmouth motorway witnessed by Finian Handley, Sept. 26, 2008.** The objects reportedly changed from lights into large triangles, briefly became transparent and displayed contrasting movement patterns. It illustrates the recurring triangle motif and high-evidence direct witness testimony.
- **mctxNflysVQ, Part 3: 20th Anniversary NPC.** The card gathers seven encounters and explicitly frames the Cash-Landrum case through competing claims about extraterrestrial and human craft. It illustrates how coverup narratives, misidentification and crash-retrieval claims overlap.
- **mWbWQfviRU4, Numerous sightings of large UFOs observed by UK policemen.** Gary Esletine's database is described as containing over 425 cases involving 940 officers, with examples near nuclear facilities. It illustrates the attempt to build institutional witness networks.
