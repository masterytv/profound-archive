- **Contact as benevolent versus contact as harmful:** Aurora Belcea reports telepathic reassurance and no pain, whereas Thom Reed describes restraint, severe pain, fear, and beings showing no concern for him. (S_2ukWbGkZo; SYoXSa32EZI)
