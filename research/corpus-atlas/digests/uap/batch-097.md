## Tensions and contradictions

- The batch alternates between accounts presenting UAP as peaceful or reassuring and accounts emphasizing fear or possible aggression. Rosane reports encouragement from telepathic beings, and the mantis being reassures the child, while Glenn describes a creature becoming aggressive and striking his truck, and Richard Dolan cautions that not all encounters are peaceful. V_Be2WVl1KI, VkIiluhsI30, VkAC5M4eNj0, VZ5TzFnAPRY.
- Some accounts emphasize reporting, witnesses and institutional response, while others describe missing records or deliberate non-reporting. The USS Franklin D. Roosevelt witnesses sought corroboration despite absent ship logs, whereas the Navy allegedly avoided reporting McGwier's incidents and the Westall witnesses allegedly faced pressure to remain silent. Ve5s9Ql-M5Q, VmcHDOz13ec, VZOATGGOUrc.
- The evidentiary register is divided between physical or operational effects and highly speculative cosmological claims. Nuclear-system failures and a submerged object are presented as incident testimony, while the artificial-moon and sun-stealing scenarios are explicitly theoretical or conspiratorial. Vn_Kuh3UPrk, VmcHDOz13ec, VgwOI_IMOu0, VmA2AW0i5r8.
