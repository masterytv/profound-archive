## Tensions and contradictions

- Some cards present high-evidence, multi-witness sightings, while others emphasize ambiguity or ordinary explanations. Phoenix Lights and the Roach family accounts report many witnesses and official reporting, whereas the Afghanistan video analysis suggests a drone, jet or lens reflection. (ihqVIu5SSEg, ifCd7nFeSX8, ieFzeyLW608)
- Contact narratives vary from severe bodily consequences to no transformation. Antonio La Rubia reported persistent illness after an alleged examination, while Donald Shrum's encounter included entities and temporary loss of consciousness but no lasting transformation rating. (i_f0dE8yyA8, ieOQHMcGUuk)
- Disclosure is framed both as necessary public accountability and as likely to fail. Greer argues secrecy threatens national security, while Richard Dolan expects fragmented public disbelief even if disclosure occurs. (ifV5xTzQOwg, inaGL5cYW2g)
- The cards disagree over evidentiary status of biological claims: Nazca researchers describe compelling specimens, while the mummies are also presented as controversial and insufficiently supported. (ikNwboNQLfw, ihAMq_i2HO4)
