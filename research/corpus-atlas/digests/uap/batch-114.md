- The batch alternates between claims of strong evidence and explicit uncertainty. The Vandenberg account receives evidence 17 and contact 18 in aVGaUHpPXoI, while the 13 Atlas discussion says there is no solid proof of an artificial object in aZe_aAoJ76M.
