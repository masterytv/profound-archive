## Tensions and contradictions

- Some accounts frame UAPs as non-human visitors or intelligences, while others argue that the central phenomenon is a hidden control system regulating consciousness rather than alien visitation (kRO5jOa06Qw, kfqUWOnibhg, kSrKGS_UrhI).
- Steven Greer argues that the main threat is man-made UAPs and disinformation, while other disclosure cards present recovered craft and non-human biologics as evidence of extraterrestrial technology (katZjHUmBh0, kZle-PYmoQE, kRO5jOa06Qw).
- Skinwalker Ranch is presented as an evidence-focused investigation, yet its reported phenomena include entities, psi content and harmful incidents that are difficult to standardize or independently verify (kNEJaw-Erks, kcuKgG0YLsE).
- Several cases emphasize physical traces or technical effects, such as bent antennas and failed instruments, while other reports consist mainly of lights, orbs or interpretive claims without physical evidence (kUNI3u-5o4A, kPu2mdKE00o, kR2VQ54otfQ, kYola2O62og).
