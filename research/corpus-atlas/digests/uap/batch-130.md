- Military and nuclear-site encounters, about 7 of 40. Reports connect UAP activity with bases, missiles, communications, weapons systems and recovered objects. Examples: fzvwBBSmWYA, g28EbphgcG0.
- Close encounters, abduction and missing time, about 12 of 40. Accounts include onboard experiences, examinations, paralysis, hypnosis and altered time perception. Examples: g4jmowBtrAY, gDcMUwhtFWA.
