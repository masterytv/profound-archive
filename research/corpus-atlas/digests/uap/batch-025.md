## Tensions and contradictions

- Some witnesses and researchers favor extraterrestrial or nonhuman explanations, including the alleged occupants in 7Jc2G5aEH0A and the extraterrestrial interpretation in 7MTRqq8w008. By contrast, David Allen believed his triangular objects were probably top-secret American aircraft, and Clas Swan reports that most Swedish sightings have mundane explanations. (7OArRx0qgH8, 7RLtpVR_mdA)
- Institutional responses vary sharply. Marcel reports being ordered to use the weather-balloon explanation, while Drabier's superiors reportedly called the sighting a mass hallucination. (7STUNCWWn5c, 7GZQhBTFdxo) Eileen Morland's account, however, received genuine attention from authorities, and several military-linked cases were formally reported. (7RyuKYc17yE, 7RzzvD6rlVw)
- Contact ranges from zero-contact observation, as coded for Kelly Chase's sighting, to high-contact telepathic or onboard accounts involving Fred Zewe, Sean Cahill, and the cases summarized by Preston Dennett. (7Lu-opFB4dA, 7LPhbvpnqEM, 7UByNHwX8E8, 7LYoKQgOE4g)
- The batch presents disclosure as both increasing and obstructed. Panels describe greater media and academic seriousness, while other cards allege secrecy, false reports, hidden funding, or continuing government nontransparency. (7Ssp3IuZLJ4, 7TENI5cN8K4, 7M5BkOgJ0V4)
