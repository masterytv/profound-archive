## Tensions and contradictions

- The batch contains accounts of secrecy, reprisals, and withheld medical records, including claims that whistleblowers face threats and that records were classified (WHEmJ07pCYA, WCDVIGxn48M). Against this, Oscar Santa María's sighting was officially accepted without negative career consequences, and he continued his military career (WJjP0khrKsI).
- Some accounts portray contact as frightening, destabilizing, or damaging, including Bennewitz's paranoia and Márcio Couxa's injuries (WBTqsbwJyqU, WIwHmAMlprc). Others describe contact as benevolent, purposeful, or emotionally positive, especially Deb Shakti's experiences and Dan Harari's messages about benevolent aliens (WI90tEMz3oc, WIzrOxVOQVA).
- The evidence posture varies sharply: Edwin Fuhr reports detailed physical craft observation and an investigated failure of his equipment (WBQXeZMLRLI), while several disclosure claims concern hardware or programs the speaker did not personally see, including Dave's account (W8PkzA0WWxk).
