## Standout accounts

- FgdFJse9M30, "I Saw a UFO Interfere with Nuclear Missiles." Mario Woods describes missile-control anomalies, reports of UFOs near missile sites, and national-security implications. It illustrates the military-secrecy theme, though the card says he did not always personally see the objects.
- FmxiD1NY2tY, "Alien Experiencers - From Fear To Communication During Contact - The Movie." Nick Cornelison reports seeing a human-featured being as a child, followed by secrecy, curiosity, and long-term transformation. It illustrates the contact and transformation theme. 
- FsXVLxD9szI, "UFOs Exposed: Mario Woods' Pentagon Whistleblower Testimony." Woods describes signaling a bright light near a nuclear silo and seeing it respond before a missile-site alarm. It is a particularly clear example of an alleged interaction between a craft and strategic military infrastructure.
- G4YCVNZ7WIM, "First interview with Travis Walton after his alien abduction experience, 1975." The account combines a close-range craft encounter, loss of consciousness, an apparent onboard setting, human-like beings, and subsequent confusion. It illustrates the abduction and crash-retrieval narrative cluster.
- G18QonIvl3w, "Anne Watson: The Zeta Grey Who Unzipped Me." The card combines astral travel, extraterrestrial healing, an out-of-body experience involving Jesus, a hybrid program, and galactic meditation. It represents the batch's most expansive spiritual-contact framing.
- G30zCVFR5SY, "Rep. Tim Burchett on underwater bases, whistleblowers and Trump." Burchett relays claims from pilots and a former admiral about fast underwater objects and football-field-sized sonar detections. It illustrates how USO claims enter political disclosure discourse.
- FzSPgtsxD0M, "Psionic Asset - Michael Battista." Battista reports a blue orb, a blue mist containing a transparent cube, daily sightings, and a struggle with reality after repeated encounters. It illustrates the overlap between physical observation, psi content, and personal transformation.

- Claims of systematic concealment contrast with accounts that remain cautious or nonconfirmatory. Woods describes secrecy and missile interference, while the E2 pilot did not confirm seeing aliens and the Arrow-report commentary says no alien technology was recovered.
