## Tensions and contradictions

- Accounts differ on whether institutions are beginning to disclose or still actively suppressing information. Gillibrand's advocacy for reporting protections and a specialized office suggests reform, while the Wilson Davis notes, redacted UK files and Greer's claims emphasize continued secrecy and lack of oversight. (fQS5xLCNQI8, fSR87g5zy6Q, fOoemA2s1os, f_RA6Olulnc)
- Contact is framed as benevolent and potentially healing in fR_sv_hIO84, fSrnynOlW1o and fadaRPcldXI, but as invasive, painful or terrifying in fQzjLUp7lSo, fSN34FcvQ1Y and fZSR5-EY2Ro.
- Transformation is not consistent. Some experiencers describe awakening, research commitments or blessing, while Ry describes fear and feeling unsafe at home, and Linda Napolitano experiences trauma and paranoia. (fVzXDXJtLwo, fSrnynOlW1o, fV6Q0LpgbLQ, fZSR5-EY2Ro)
- The batch mixes direct military testimony with retold or documentary cases. Kenneth Storch and Graham Bethune are presented as direct experiencers, whereas Linda Napolitano's account is narrated and Johnny Cash's story is retold, creating different evidentiary distances. (fOwS-7Fl114, fU6LOfiUJ6Q, fZSR5-EY2Ro, fYfxyUHY-Ug)
