## Themes

- Disclosure, secrecy and institutional conflict, about 15 of 40. Cards repeatedly frame UAPs through alleged government suppression, classified programs, congressional oversight, legal action and Area 51, including p266szAgnls, p6CRiXjK9us, pAB8nwGv2VU, pJEBpBCyWF8.
- Consciousness, telepathy and transformation, about 14 of 40. Remote viewing, meditation, altered states and changed worldviews connect encounters to nonlocal communication or spiritual development, as in p41LmFcH3jc, p0obOViL1eQ, pETCwRwTync and pC6HtTT7hx8.
- Abduction and humanoid contact, about 10 of 40. Missing time, bedroom visitations, hypnosis, onboard experiences and named entities recur in p5BQ-oQQrpY, p6DqLWs4Qo4, pF76WlZc0QY and pJw3P41ZtRs.
- Physical traces and effects, about 12 of 40. Reports include burns, electromagnetic interference, vehicle effects, radar tracking, missile disruptions and physical landing evidence, represented by p5BQ-oQQrpY, p6HTah_WZG8, p6vM5DeAhXU and pK_HhJUYhSY.
- Research legitimacy and community formation, about 8 of 40. Several cards call for methodological rigor, academic cooperation, public platforms or unity among researchers, including p5KtrEvQwpM, pCTo-wUPnzQ, pBlYafhDRQg and pFHHxzoIuy8.

- Contact is framed as benevolent consciousness evolution in p0obOViL1eQ and as peace, healing and collective meditation in pETCwRwTync, but as terrifying, physically invasive or traumatic in p5BQ-oQQrpY, p5OvEJ5XVy0 and pJw3P41ZtRs.
- Some cards present disclosure as approaching through legal, congressional or institutional channels, including p6CRiXjK9us and pAB8nwGv2VU. Others emphasize disappointment, insufficient data and skepticism about current disclosure, especially pBlYafhDRQg and pFHHxzoIuy8.
- The phenomenon is treated as extraterrestrial in p0obOViL1eQ and p6RFN3wiJ-E, while pETCwRwTync explicitly separates extraterrestrial, interdimensional and man-made craft.
- Research cards call for academic methods and collaboration in pCTo-wUPnzQ and p5KtrEvQwpM, while several narratives rely on hypnosis, remote viewing or altered states, as in p5BQ-oQQrpY, p41LmFcH3jc and pFzSrxmd4Vs.
