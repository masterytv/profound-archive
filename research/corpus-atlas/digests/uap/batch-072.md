## Standout accounts

- **N2Uk2RZHZQs, 1947 UFO crash & alien bodies eyewitness Gerald Anderson interviewed by Stanton Friedman.** A family account combines a crashed craft, four non-human bodies, military threats, and a connection to the Roswell narrative. It strongly illustrates the secrecy and crash-retrieval themes.
- **NEsOQCwt4yw, Glowing Sphere UFO Over Nuclear Weapons on USS JFK.** A glowing sphere was reportedly seen above a Navy ship during communications and later radar failures, followed by instructions to keep the event classified. It links military infrastructure, physical effects, and institutional silence.
- **N7-a-_gx_yU, Green Halo Over Rome.** Captain Poggi reports a green light passing beneath a commercial aircraft, a sudden jump, and earlier unusual aerial observations. It illustrates pilot testimony and the recurring association between UAP reports and aviation risk.
- **N6MOVZFKKWc, The MOST AMAZING Story Of Healing By Aliens!** Marie Lopez describes a bright room, vibrations, voices in an unknown language, and the disappearance of stomach pain. It is a concentrated example of healing, entity contact, and high transformation.
- **NFoG3OVq3e4, The 2013 UAP Citizen Hearing.** Rendlesham Forest witnesses describe unusual lights, static electricity, sanitized reports, and classification pressure. It illustrates how testimony becomes a dispute over both the event and the official record.
- **N8Auf7PXMak, Man Goes Missing in Most Disturbing Alien Abduction Case Yet!** Heriberto Garza's reported abduction includes a named origin world, blood drawing, telepathic voices, apparent bodily change, and later disappearance. It is unusually dense in claims of biological and post-encounter consequences.
