- Disclosure, secrecy and institutional accountability, about 14 of 40. Cards repeatedly frame UAPs through congressional hearings, withheld information, crash retrievals, classified programs and contested official explanations. Examples: fjFGLL68fxw, fpK3Sg2Qggs.
- Direct observation of unusual lights or craft, about 16 of 40. Witnesses describe hovering discs, pulsating lights, black circles, objects near aircraft and lights splitting into orbs. Examples: fbrN_dqSdJ8, fr8Ryztay6k.
