## Themes

- Disclosure, secrecy and institutional control, about 18 of 40. Accounts describe deleted emails, suppressed crash material, censorship, classified briefings and pressure for transparency. Examples: FD_AQiW15nI, F5P04uI2hu0.
- Consciousness, telepathy and intentional contact, about 20 of 40. Psi appears in both individual encounters and organized CE5 or consciousness programs, including group coherence, telepathic communication and claimed star-family contact. Examples: F56P24aulLY, F8LRw3WajCM.
- Occupants, abduction and bodily intervention, about 14 of 40. Greys, mantis beings, humanoids, hybrids and other entities are linked to procedures, examinations, reproductive claims or onboard experiences. Examples: F4glUvG8SMc, F6mJaegHKb0.
- Military proximity and operational risk, about 10 of 40. UAPs appear near bases, aircraft, restricted airspace and military operations, sometimes with radar, instrument or tracking failures. Examples: FD_AQiW15nI, FGmmz4wtMbw.
- Historical continuity and cross-case patterning, about 12 of 40. Cards connect cases across decades and countries, especially through crash-retrieval narratives, recurring humanoid reports and proposed geographic patterns. Examples: FGiwgyLY7Aw, FDwQvodc92U.
