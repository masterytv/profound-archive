## Themes

- Return as assignment: about 28 of 40 accounts involve being told, choosing, or compelled to return because work, family, purpose, or unfinished tasks remain. Examples include 5RLxuX_ktAU, 5U5VhcILuxk, 5l_68PqP_DQ.
- Love, peace, and homecoming: about 30 of 40 describe profound peace, unconditional love, welcome, or belonging, often associated with light or a benevolent presence. Examples: 5ZfaPCwjguk, 5h-w2vGvq8s.
- Life review and moral consequence: about 20 of 40 include a life review or reflection on how actions affected others. This is explicit in 5RLxuX_ktAU, 5XrA79_T_R0, and 5h-w2vGvq8s.
- Unity and expanded consciousness: about 20 of 40 report oneness, energy, interconnectedness, knowledge downloads, or reality beyond the physical body. Examples: 5RSnbaTYtWY, 5gWvdXWjIek, 5hW_JoJY0WM.
- Hell and distress as a transitional landscape: about 8 of 40 include darkness, demons, torture, hell, or spiritual warfare before rescue or return. Examples: 5SGbWUWoP8Y, 5aRngG7_45w, 5oJbYGzDV3Q.

- 5XrA79_T_R0, The near-death experience of Peter Anthony. A full 15-element account combines surgical observation, deceased people, mathematical knowledge, a panoramic life review, and high evidential-strength scoring. It illustrates the maximal "complete NDE" pattern.
- 5gWvdXWjIek, IANDS NoVA - Nanci Danison 2021.11.21. The account describes 15 elements, past lives, reality testing, Source as energy, and information downloads. It illustrates expanded cosmology and the difficulty of separating reported experience from interpretation.
- 5SGbWUWoP8Y, Dies. Sees Souls Arriving from Different Religions. A hellish realm gives way to compassion, a figure of light, and a heavenly waiting room where everyone can escape hell. It illustrates mixed journeys and religious pluralism.
