## Tensions and contradictions

- Return is sometimes an explicit choice, as in 5--x0cNpeKo and 5LYzsuQNHnI, but elsewhere it is imposed or described as a forceful extraction, as in 4xx51AptzbM and 5FgjHWPvMPo.
- Religious interpretation varies sharply: 52EDJNFlOiw presents Jesus, atonement and scripture, while 5Ko_0NXnjC8 presents an abstract, language-transcending experience without a clear religious figure.
- Hell and punitive judgment appear in 4zHBfcT4ylA, whereas 5CJl_PY-EKg frames suffering as chosen limitation within a larger structure of love and free will.
- Evidential strength is uneven. 52e1gAdDH-Y is rated 27 and 5CJl_PY-EKg 21, while 52ZvFUMf4hk and 58QNyS8MoQQ explicitly lack verifiable perceptions and are rated 7.
- Some accounts describe extensive NDE histories but provide few detected elements, notably 5BeSkfVUvBw, which claims 60 NDEs but lists only two elements, compared with the detailed 14-element account in 5CJl_PY-EKg.
