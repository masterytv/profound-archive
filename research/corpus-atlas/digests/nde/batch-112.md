## Themes

- Return as obligation or choice, about 31 of 40. Children, family, purpose, or an explicit command motivate return in accounts including grqOz1znEyk, gxU34Htvjl0, hC73DmSCsyk, and hCJAv1TTPLw.
- Love, peace, and heightened reality, about 32 of 40. The cards repeatedly describe overwhelming calm, unconditional love, vivid perception, and a world felt more real, including gpPurcd_ERU and gsq72npuxFs.
- Life review as moral education, about 15 of 40. Reviews emphasize emotional consequences, forgiveness, and changed conduct in gogkn8gA41g, gr2Lksx6lJ4, h0p77xQ7akI, and hAlNjEiMo4A.
- Guides, Jesus, God, or luminous beings, about 27 of 40. Religious figures dominate many accounts, while other cards describe nonreligious guides or anonymous presences, including h-hb8Mj47N0 and h2lf_Tx3_dk.
- Lasting transformation, about 29 of 40. Reduced death fear, increased spirituality, compassion, and purpose recur after both NDEs and related experiences, including grTtRgeryqM, h1cT-uX6k2g, and hDLrwbFCyUI.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Return is often framed as a personal choice, as in grqOz1znEyk, gxU34Htvjl0, and hC73DmSCsyk, but some accounts describe an authoritative command or forced return, including gorIlgxeHXs, h6HA9EjP7bo, and gyAB-mFUdhM.
- The afterlife is usually loving and peaceful, but gtvHYeQD4hg and h1uEl6Fi9sQ include hellish or hostile phases before contact with Jesus or spiritual rescue.
- Some accounts present Jesus in recognizable human or religious form, including gpPurcd_ERU and h6HA9EjP7bo, while gsq72npuxFs describes a colorful electric Creator and grTtRgeryqM reports pure awareness without a clearly personal deity.
- Transformation is prominent in many cards, but gorIlgxeHXs and gvgoHeurDXo explicitly report no transformation discussed, despite vivid experiences.
