## Themes

- Love, light, and spiritual unity, about 30 of 40. Many accounts describe an overwhelming loving presence, bright source, or oneness, including PbSZgTaJp2A and PpY5ltV78gQ.
- Choice, purpose, and return, about 30 of 40. Experiencers are commonly told to return because of children, unfinished work, or an earthly task, as in PnbK-8jddWM and Q3qUinRBTVo.

- Hell versus unconditional welcome: PdIXHExitkw describes total absence of God, joy, and peace in a hell-like void, while Pdbj2oz58E describes misery followed by an offered path into light. By contrast, PpY5ltV78gQ emphasizes acceptance, forgiveness, and being a child of God.
- Voluntary versus forced return: PbSZgTaJp2A and PwKRiyk4PNQ describe choosing to return, while PcUzlmOUIH4 and PqddJsxn16s record return as forced or imposed.
- Medical crisis versus waking encounter: PdszHuP8_2w and Q4X4SNkOspg describe cardiac-arrest out-of-body observations, while PlRS0k4rnpA reports an after-death encounter during ordinary waking activity without medical compromise.
- Immediate versus delayed transformation: PzzSmejFYlc turns its experience into compassionate action, while PqddJsxn16s says the experience initially had no impact and the experiencer remained an atheist for 35 years.
