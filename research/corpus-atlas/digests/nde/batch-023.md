## Standout accounts

- 8Vetsv4BJIE, "She Fell Out Of A Bus, Was RUN OVER, And Went Through A Portal - Near Death Experience (NDE)." This is the only card with all 15 standard elements present. Its aquatic planet, remembered nonhuman identity, portals, Yeshua, and negotiated return illustrate the batch's expansive cosmology theme.
- 8iNucPVPGw4, "Two Minutes of Eternity - An Extraordinary Near Death Experience - Bo Katzman's NDE." The account combines a full life review, timelessness, omniscience, telepathy, and an attempted observation of doctors during surgery. It illustrates the tension between inner revelation and claims of physical perception.
- 8eJQg9zfo1w, "The near death experience of Ray Kinman." A child becomes a sphere of light, meets his deceased dog Skippy, and receives instantaneous universal knowledge. It strongly illustrates childhood NDEs, cosmic unity, and nonhuman deceased companions.
- 8hLPgdfk9EM, "Woman Dies, Sees Future, Colors Beings, & Says Don't Freak Out - Powerful Near Death Experience." The suicide-attempt context is followed by a blissful life review and a son's future voice calling her back. It illustrates recovery narratives in which family obligation becomes the return mechanism.
- 8dWMZU4F1OA, "I Ended Up In An Enormous, Endless Room, Luminous And White." Two blue-skinned, fish-tailed beings communicate that the experiencer was not meant to have children in this life. It illustrates rare nonhuman entities and destiny claims within an otherwise peaceful NDE.
- 8YGDBN9X7R8, "People Who Saw Hell...and Found a Way Out." This is the clearest hellish account, moving through terror, pain, and hopelessness before turning toward God, light, and healing. It complicates the batch's overwhelmingly positive emotional pattern.
- 8cnODbcgbBg, "NDE TV Presents 'The Will of a Wildflower' by Pegi Robinson, available on Amazon books (Part One)." The card contains no transcript and no analyzable NDE sequence. It stands out as a source-quality limitation rather than an experience, and should not be treated as evidence for thematic counts.
