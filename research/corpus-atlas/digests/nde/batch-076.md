## Themes
- Return as a negotiated or imposed decision, about 30 of 40. Experiencers commonly resist, choose family or purpose, or are explicitly sent back, as in TfLLY_HdWT8 and ThW7SB7FmFA.
- Love, peace and relief from bodily pain, about 30 of 40. These states recur across medical crises, accidents and childhood events, including TsLJzfXVrtc and U1VxK7n8ccQ.
- Expanded consciousness and unity, about 25 of 40. Accounts describe timelessness, universal connection, information downloads or identity beyond the body, especially Tebz0cidZds and TvZHlmMaMwA.

## Tensions and contradictions
- Return is sometimes chosen for children, family or unfinished work, as in Td0AAHSsz5E and TzdiBBZuKNw, but other accounts describe forced return or explicit refusal, including ThW7SB7FmFA and TueuzsvKDiE.
- Most journeys are peaceful or loving, yet Tgw6G8DINL4 includes frightening entities and TlvIHuRZ-V4 begins in a black abyss with hopelessness. TnrlbvHAzi8E presents emotional crisis as a hell-like state that transforms into unity.
- Some accounts report strongly organized religious figures, such as Jesus in TlvIHuRZ-V4 and Archangel Michael in Td0AAHSsz5E, while Tebz0cidZds and TjlFrgS_53Y describe consciousness, timelessness and interconnectedness without a stable denominational frame.
- Evidential strength varies sharply. TsOGyCidXLQ, TqUf2YJ6GSE, TsDTsBCEd6A and TueuzsvKDiE receive high ratings, while TlRdS1pFUuI and U0-6LEiJd7c contain sparse subjective material and no specific verifiable perception claims.
