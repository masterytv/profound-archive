- The batch commonly depicts death as peaceful, loving, and nonthreatening, especially 7X3ydhGNhDI and 7SW-1rKQIrg, but distressing or punitive imagery appears in 7Rp2RQrGAKo and 7XvpEI_loZE, including fear, judgment, demons, hell, or being left behind.
- Return is often chosen or accepted, as in 7EMRdPe4_lU and 7N5wR77PfXA, but several accounts describe forced return or an external command, including 7El1Lx6pBYY and 7TfFeukn10I.
- Some accounts emphasize visual landscapes and entities, such as 7J3Q19mf9Ow and 7MEH_fdNgEo, while 7X3ydhGNhDI reports no visuals and primarily intense feelings.
- The cards vary sharply in evidential strength: 7YfKmJYfHyI is rated 25, while 7Zsp5nvsMpw contains only "Hi" and has no NDE material.
