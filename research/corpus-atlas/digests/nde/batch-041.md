## Standout accounts

- **FoABYf_kZps, "THE UTTERLY ASTONISHING THINGS I SAW during my NDE."** The card detects all 15 standard elements, including a life review, guides, a healing pool and cosmic unity. It is a maximal example of the light, knowledge and transformation themes.
- **Fxl-nRX5Lrs, "The Near-Death Experience of Rene Volken."** The wife appears in the clothes from her coffin and gives a specific instruction to return for the children. The card assigns Veridical 23, making this a key account for evidential claims and bereavement.
- **FqXPVHFa7cw, "The Near Death Experience of Jana A. Hermann."** The experiencer follows the spirit of her deceased premature child and encounters her grandmother at a boundary. It illustrates childbirth trauma, family reunion and separation.
- **Fp6BAVniAVA, "Man Died And Was Taken To Hell And Heaven."** This account includes demons, torture, lonely souls, a blocked garden and eventual joy in light. It complicates the overwhelmingly positive journey pattern.
- **G5PUdVr-k98, "I Died, Met Aliens & Saw The Future Of Earth."** The narrative expands beyond familiar religious imagery into alien planets, telepathic beings and future Earth claims. It illustrates cosmological breadth and speculative prophecy.
- **G5bNTXajs2E, "It Will Leave You Speechless - He Died And Was Shown His Multiple Past Lives."** The card describes numerous past lives, deceased pets and collective consciousness. It is a strong example of reincarnation and expanded identity.
- **GBlrSg8GWV8, "Man Dies During a Severe Storm, Then Guided Back into His Body."** The experiencer becomes part of a community of light beings, undergoes a life review and argues about returning. It combines reunion, moral review and reluctant mission.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Most journeys are peaceful and loving, but Fp6BAVniAVA begins with demonic figures and hellish scenes, while G2zW7fMs3LE begins in terror before becoming a conversion narrative.
- Return is often framed as a freely chosen mission, as in FoABYf_kZps and Fq1qdoLA1fo, but other accounts describe forced or externally commanded return, as in G78BwAa5fTo and GBlrSg8GWV8.
- Deceased relatives are usually comforting, as in Fxl-nRX5Lrs and FvajKZf1u5Q, yet FqXPVHFa7cw presents reunion with a dead child through an irreversible boundary.
- Several accounts claim unusually strong evidential access, especially FmIl7pMegsI and Fxl-nRX5Lrs, while many cards explicitly say the experience is subjective and lacks verifiable perception, including FmXIA_65tqk and G3eLsmzqCV8.
- The archive groups an ADC, an STE and an OBE with NDE material: FvajKZf1u5Q, GA-uaddBobA, FvtSVrlaUVU and G5pKcEwxx9Q. Two cards contain no usable transcript, FnmzIAj1XMI and G8cBlKDj1Gk.
