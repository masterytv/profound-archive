## Standout accounts

- **mUzS5gHTAvI, A Man Blind from Birth Describes Heaven in Near Death Experience.** Brad Burrow, blind from birth, reports visual perception during his NDE, including traffic and trolleys, followed by a tunnel, meadow, music, and an angelic figure. - **maINoqE7aZA, Blind - But Able to See During a Near-Death Experience.** Mrs. Egrigoz, blind since childhood, describes seeing her mother knit a red jumper and receiving a symbolic ring from Reini. The account illustrates veridical-style perception, family connection, and a promise to return.
- **mchb-BU_Tew, Woman Dies; Reveals The Incredible Truth About Israel Hamas,Only a Few People On Earth Know About It.** Linda Kramer describes prolonged time distortion, telepathic communication with nature, becoming other beings, and a life review involving memory bubbles. It expands the standard NDE vocabulary toward identity fluidity and planetary consciousness.
- **mYQW6JR1vPs, I visited the 3 heavens when I died.** Howard Pittman describes a second heaven populated by demons and a third heaven where God condemns his faith as inadequate. It illustrates judgment, spiritual warfare, and a strongly corrective transformation.
- **mYhBQeOv28Y, Man Dies; Reveals FUTURE DISASTER Through Lucid Dream!** Charlie Moy's distressing experience includes a tunnel, life photographs, a choice to live, PTSD symptoms, and later therapeutic confrontation with a death-dwarf in lucid dreams. It illustrates that NDE aftermath can be traumatic rather than consoling.
- **mZj9eWeccAM, EXPERIENCIA QUASE MORTE- Ela tinha cancer terminal.** A physician reports a terminal cancer diagnosis followed by an NDE and a later PET scan showing no cancer. The card labels the account low in evidential strength, making it useful for examining healing claims versus archival verification.
- **mkWYL4Ufw7s, Elisa's Life Review & Choice to Go Back.** Alisa Palmer's account combines all 15 detected elements with a life review, future images, familiar figures, and a voluntary return to complete a mission. It is a concentrated example of the archive's full NDE pattern.
