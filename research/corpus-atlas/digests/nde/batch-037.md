## Standout accounts

- DxXXPVPiAaQ, "What Happens When We Die? Evidence That Changes Everything (Full Documentary)". It is scored 15/15 for elements and Veridical 20, combining body observation, tunnel, deceased-relative contact, and a return command. It illustrates the batch's fullest conventional NDE pattern.
- E7OAcX3AKCA, "NDE TV Presents Brian , a therapist dead for 10 minutes, has a very detailed Near Death Experience." The account has Veridical 23, 14/15 elements, a life review, Jesus, and a stated return choice after cardiac arrest. It illustrates the strongest evidentially rated account in this batch.
- EHwBXZXzByM, "From Death to Divine: Karen’s Life-Changing Near-Death Experience". The operating-room account includes a telepathic instruction about a rushing man later identified as the surgeon, with Veridical 23. It illustrates claims of externally checkable perception during surgery.
- E24GWdoYxwg, "One of The MOST DETAILED Near Death Experiences You Might Ever Hear". The experiencer reports a heart stoppage, a heavenly threshold, an infant-death revelation, and returning after perceiving a nurse's grief. It illustrates compassion as the mechanism of return.
- E4qZlc17IyY, "NDE: My Life-Altering Near-Death Experience - Meeting Kisi Manito". A Shadow Spirit, hell-like darkness, Machi, and Kizi Manitou produce the batch's clearest Indigenous spiritual and adversarial framing. It illustrates a strongly distressing counter-pattern.
- EJbEa_ZYL3s, "The Out-of-Body Experiences of Ms. Zürcher". This is classified as an obe rather than a conventional NDE and includes repeated journeys, visited apartments, and reported verification of names and layouts. It illustrates continuity between spontaneous OBE and evidential claims.
- E-4gkn_RWsc, "Teenager DIES, FINDS The SECRET To HEALING". A 16-year-old's alcohol-poisoning NDE features seven angels, a 30-foot compassionate being, sacred geometry, and sound healing. It illustrates healing knowledge presented as a post-NDE vocation.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Most accounts describe welcoming love and peace, including DxXXPVPiAaQ and E-IBPltVcL0, while E4qZlc17IyY and EGyosGOdxNU describe hell-like spaces, fear, demons, or condemnation.
- Return is commonly accepted or chosen for family and purpose, as in EHwBXZXzByM and E7OaKzbPWgI, but EGrpt5KgwH4 reports, "No way. Uh-uh. I am not going back," and EBQVdBVS3Xs says the experiencer did not want to return.
- Life reviews are frequently framed as compassionate learning, as in E7OAcX3AKCA and ECEzjV5Pxac, but E3F2a0r2F9w presents repeated tests, temptations, violence, and confrontations.
- High evidential ratings accompany specific perception claims in E7OAcX3AKCA and EHwBXZXzByM, whereas many vivid accounts explicitly lack veridical claims, including DubxkcrXgJU and ECy75JJHl0E.
