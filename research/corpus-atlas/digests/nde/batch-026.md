## Themes

- Love, unity and expanded spiritual identity, about 28 of 40. Experiencers repeatedly describe unconditional love, cosmic connection, or dissolution of separation, including 9mVngUk7v0U, A0mzdF9pqMU, and A1onYxA8wiY.
- A return assignment or negotiated choice, about 29 of 40. Beings commonly say the experiencer has more work, family obligations, or a message to deliver, as in 9miac9B9XCo, 9uQPuelkQT0, and 9wctnpGIK_4.
- Life review as moral and relational learning, about 20 of 40. Reviews involve emotional consequences rather than simple punishment, notably 9m77ID3wC6s, 9tez3zAEL3I, and A2yVhtUXtvE.
- Reduced fear of death and major transformation, about 25 of 40. The strongest recorded transformations include scores of 40 in 9oIfs0RVqqw and 9tkkQdBqerw, while many accounts emphasize purpose, compassion, and spiritual awareness.
- Encounters with deceased relatives, animals, guides, or religious figures, about 25 of 40. Family reunions appear in 9trbbBC4sA0 and 9oNvs75DDEk, while animal reunions appear in 9orre2SMJIA and A4m8xYkPcp0.
- Hellish or distressing environments, about 6 of 40. These range from demons and torture in 9s9mn9OCyN0 and A3V1ccODlTM to a magma-filled hell in A2NGV591_Tw.

## Standout accounts

- 9m77ID3wC6s, "Near-Death Experience of a Combat Soldier | He Met Jesus | NDE." This is one of the highest-scoring accounts, with Greyson 30, intensity 9, and 14 elements. Its thought-based life review and explicit Jesus encounter illustrate religious interpretation joined to the recurring love and return-assignment themes.
- 9mVngUk7v0U, "More Descriptions of the Dying Process (19 NDE Stories)." The card assigns all 15 elements and intensity 10, including panoramic perception, unconditional love, knowledge download, and time distortion. It illustrates the fullest version of the standard NDE pattern, although it is a compilation rather than one clearly isolated account.
- 9mf_ak6pquc, "A Crowd CHEERED As She Entered Heaven." Kaye's account links an NDE to gender affirmation, divine acceptance, and a post-experience public mission. The card records Transformation 38 and a being who says she is not damned, illustrating identity, salvation, and social transformation.
- 9mv-Wt7LLhM, "30 Years of NDE Exploration: Unveiling the Mind-Blowing Truth." This shared death experience centers on a deceased mother collecting her dying child while the living witness remains bilocated. It illustrates grief, reunion, and the boundary between NDE and SDE.
- 9oIfs0RVqqw, "She Drown And Was Shown The Truth About Free Will And Choice." A child enters the minds of rescuers and receives a future-life account, with Greyson 30, Transformation 40, and Veridical 22. It strongly illustrates telepathy, agency, and purpose.
- 9s9mn9OCyN0, "Kelly Ashford: From Hell to Heaven and Back." This is a highly distressing counter-pattern involving a dungeon, demons, torture, life review, and later heaven. Its positive tone despite a distressing journey complicates the assumption that emotional valence is uniform throughout an experience.
- A1TZ2U38_eM, "Near Death Experience: She Died & Talked With God." The child reports detailed perceptions during the ambulance journey and a symbolic lesson about free will, then describes return as painful. The account stands out for high Veridical 21 and its unusually childlike, coercive return negotiation.

- Return is presented as genuine choice in 9lN-gwjKSUM, 9wctnpGIK_4, and 9oIfs0RVqqw, but as forced, zapped, or commanded in 9miac9B9XCo, 9z_axo_GaVo, and A4G1BLeW-P0.
- The afterlife is usually peaceful and loving in 9oNvs75DDEk, 9rYQ8fymSn4, and A0mzdF9pqMU, but explicitly punitive or terrifying in 9s9mn9OCyN0, A3V1ccODlTM, and A2NGV591_Tw.
- Some accounts identify a personal God or Jesus, including 9m77ID3wC6s and 9wctnpGIK_4, while others describe impersonal Source, consciousness, or unity, including A0mzdF9pqMU and A2yVhtUXtvE.
- The cards distinguish evidential strength from intensity: 9nNCgpjhPZU has Veridical 22 and no transformation discussed, while 9qEzJhoWYy0 has Transformation 36 but Veridical 7 and no veridical claim.
