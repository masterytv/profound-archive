- Out-of-body perception and separation from the physical self: about 30 of 40, often involving observation of the body from above or movement through walls, ceilings, or space. Examples: 3b7HhWuIL3Q, 3j7YqTZkRuE.

## Standout accounts

- 3UejjZIuZtA, "I died twice and met a council in heaven - NDE Interview Pegi." Two separate NDEs, childhood drowning and adult internal bleeding, combine out-of-body observation, telepathy, a life review, future grief, and an unusually high Veridical rating of 23. It illustrates repeated experiences and return decisions.
- 3Vh3vAJzZbE, "NDE TV Presents Sal, 3 Near-Death Experiences from 3 heart attacks led Sal out of his depression." Three cardiac events produce a 14-element account, including bliss, life review, cosmic unity, and knowledge download, followed by a major transformation toward joy and self-love. It illustrates recurrence and cumulative change.
- 3b7HhWuIL3Q, "Woman Clinically Dies, Shown The Magnitude And Impact Of Thoughts and Behavior." The account combines operating-room observation, apparent telepathy of a rescuer's thoughts, deceased relatives, and a moral life review. Its Veridical rating is 22, making it a central example for evidential claims.
- 3h4J81PfSbQ, "I DIED and flew around with angels - NDE / Near Death Experience." The experiencer reports leaving through the ceiling, viewing the hospital from above, flying with angels, and later confirmation by satellite imagery. It illustrates extraordinary sensory vividness and the archive's moderate Veridical rating of 14.
- 3aHSOCxbiuY, "Ice Climber Freezes to DEATH; LOSES Faith After Near-Death Experience (NDE)." A dark void leads to a light being, cosmic unity, painful awareness of harm done to others, and the batch's highest Transformation score, 40. It illustrates compassion as the core of life review.
- 3ogvhI-tcdk, "Guardian Angels Changed My Life After Near-Death Experience #nde." Giant blue beings with fish tails and feathered hair communicate telepathically that the experiencer was not meant to have a child in that incarnation. It illustrates how unusual entities coexist with familiar themes of love, peace, gratitude, and art.

## Tensions and contradictions

- The batch includes strongly hellish experiences, with Satan, demons, cold, fear, and guilt in 3U6a1aZgUwQ and 3bH946mWu18, but many other accounts describe overwhelming love, peace, and welcome, including 3XurduW72c0 and 3bUxaAN8jfY.
- Tunnel imagery is common in 3WLVfgIMHkg, 3bUxaAN8jfY, and 3j7YqTZkRuE, yet 3XurduW72c0 explicitly reports no tunnel on the outward journey, while 3erzR-tEY1Y reports darkness and voices without seeing any light.
- Return can appear voluntary, as in 3Z0udKDmfg8 and 3SGeGOIW-0Y, or imposed by a voice, guide, force, or medical rescue, as in 3eLByMuQmB8 and 3esgYXr9-LY.
- Transformation is often substantial, but 3VMvm9I6NLk and 3gb7BNBaG-M have Transformation 0 because they are research or deathbed-vision accounts, while 3aHSOCxbiuY reaches 40.
