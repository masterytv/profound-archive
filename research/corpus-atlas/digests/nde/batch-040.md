## Themes

- Return as assignment: about 30 of 40 include a return, summons, or choice to return, commonly linked to unfinished purpose, children, or helping others. Examples: FKOBI1oqfMU, FaBYL7w-Zes.
- Love, peace, and reduced fear of death: about 30 of 40 describe profound peace or love, and many report lasting acceptance of death. Examples: FHYnljT3lAU, FWZkPwiWJDE.
- Life review and moral empathy: about 18 of 40 include a life review, often experienced through the feelings of people affected rather than as external judgment. Examples: FJBZ5rEsJQM, FiSfxJZHB-s.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Return is often framed as compulsory unfinished work, as in "You need to go back" (FNUtgGdfYws), but some accounts describe agency or explicit preference, including choosing return for a child or family (FWZkPwiWJDE, FaBYL7w-Zes).
- Most accounts describe benevolent love and peace (FHYnljT3lAU, FIyz-r-ThAk), while FYZlD9MUMis presents terror, demons, punishment imagery, and a hellish realm.
- Christian figures and salvation claims dominate some narratives (FKOBI1oqfMU, FRz3o-u4mIg), whereas other accounts describe universal consciousness without a single religious authority (FJbr2VaCWZk, Fk7V4k3p3Ms).
- Several cards claim highly specific perceptions and receive high evidential ratings (FJVGdJZPIi0, FWQkPfDeaDw), while others explicitly contain no independently verifiable perception claims (FL1AzlGFQvk, Ffxx-9r7BWw).
