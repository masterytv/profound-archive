- The dominant loving light contrasts with explicitly punitive geography: divine love and peace appear in OzlfwO_zBY4, while hell, demons, and torment dominate OupepTZCUZ0.
- Several cards report specific high-strength perceptions, especially OzAHPfAx2zM and P2bz4Q9uIaQ, whereas many cards explicitly say there are no verifiable physical-world perceptions, including Ot5byhyBYHM and OsMqrwiKwc8.
- Transformation is often major, with scores of 36 in P68Pb4RJUII and PBLEP5FkYw0, but some accounts show minimal or no documented aftereffects, such as OtVY8UeVqUM and P95V4rsE1HE.
