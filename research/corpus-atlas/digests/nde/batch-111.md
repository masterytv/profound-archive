- Most accounts describe peace, love, or beauty, but gfLZD2aPo6s reports a frightening hellish realm, and gW_2PmL5mWg reports coercive evil and fear.
- Several accounts emphasize voluntary choice, including g_P2vhw5x24 and giQJREczKxw, while others describe forced or externally imposed return, including gT6gNPGXJXE and gZ0elI2Wyjo.
