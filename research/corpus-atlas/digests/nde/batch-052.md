## Themes

- Return as responsibility or assignment, about 28 of 40. Experiencers are told, choose, or decide to return because of children, unfinished work, or a mission, as in KCum9WLcy0I, KF-7DOg5aco, and KQNAmYgHmEQ.
- Love, peace, and reduced fear of death, about 30 of 40. Reports repeatedly describe overwhelming safety or unconditional love followed by changed attitudes toward death, including KE0MvdnYsRs and KHizyzEBXg4.
- Out-of-body observation, about 27 of 40. Many accounts describe seeing the body or medical scene from above, with the strongest explicit examples in KDy8rv4ka2E, KKLWW54_RXc, and KQNAmYgHmEQ.
- Life review and moral consequence, about 15 of 40. Reviews often involve feeling the effects of one's actions on other people, especially KCum9WLcy0I, KF-7DOg5aco, and KZTUVrwS3Nk.
- Deceased relatives as guides or companions, about 18 of 40. Family members commonly reassure, redirect, or accompany the experiencer, as in KErzb_cCmyA, KSveIzlXG-M, and KZZpf_QPi3Y.
- Spiritual purpose and expanded perception after the event, about 25 of 40. Accounts connect the experience to service, intuition, healing, or spiritual teaching, including KXvXjjHsXkQ and Kcd0Oltk1GA.

## Standout accounts

- KCum9WLcy0I, He Died and Was Shown How the Universe Works During NDE (Near Death Experience). This is a 15-element, Greyson 30 account combining void, light, life review, cosmic unity, parallel lives, and a deliberate return. It strongly illustrates the themes of cosmic knowledge and moral empathy.
- KDy8rv4ka2E, EQM | NDE – Ele ouvia os pensamentos. The experiencer reports hearing responders' thoughts while out of body, and the card assigns Veridical 21, the batch's second-highest listed rating. It illustrates the tension between subjective spiritual narrative and evidential claims.
- KQNAmYgHmEQ, Man Loses Half His Family In Tragic Accident. The deceased wife redirects him toward caring for their surviving son, while the account combines grief, life review, peace, cosmic unity, and a Transformation score of 38. It illustrates return as both loss and obligation.
- KS47u9TcNUQ, He Became Love - 3X Near Death Experiencer. Three reported NDEs provide repeated motifs across different ages and triggers, including future visions, a cobra encounter, and a teaching assignment. The card gives this account the batch's highest Veridical rating, 23.
- KSQ1SmGyDGw, Two NDEs from IANDS. A near-drowning account includes a 15-element profile, panoramic reverse life review, Jesus, adoptive and biological fathers, ancestors, and an explicit choice to return. It illustrates the fullest conventional NDE pattern in the batch.
- KUCVoQUHksE, Artist Dies And Is Shown The Truth About Creation. Two enormous blue-skinned beings with fish tails communicate telepathically in a watery realm, followed by a reported period of speaking an ancient, dolphin-like language. It illustrates unusually nontraditional entities and cosmology.
- KXNZqAFMDa4, Death Doula's Shared Death Experiences. The experiencer remains in the hospital room while simultaneously accompanying a dying man through a meadow and forest. It illustrates shared death experience, care, and the idea that a being of light may also be one's higher self.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Most accounts describe peace or love, but some contain fear, darkness, or hostile beings. KHizyzEBXg4 reports complete safety in light, while KEviDUwGZQw begins with a frightening smoky silhouette and KHBFh9LlycI reports demonic creatures.
- Return is usually accepted or chosen, but several experiencers resist it. KCum9WLcy0I and KF-7DOg5aco frame return as purposeful, while KbpVlW9cjhQ says the experiencer initially refused and KS47u9TcNUQ describes a promise-based return.
- Religious imagery is often Christian, but the batch also includes Buddha, Shiva-related figures, ancestor spirits, and nonhuman aquatic beings. KFyIHbORGLY names Jesus and Buddha, KG5xe8-g6rw features local ancestor and village imagery, and KUCVoQUHksE features fish-tailed beings.
- High Greyson scores do not consistently imply evidential strength. KQNAmYgHmEQ has Greyson 30 and Veridical 10, while KDy8rv4ka2E has Greyson 30 and Veridical 21; Kc9RT7hoyxY has Greyson 20 with no clear life-threatening trigger.
