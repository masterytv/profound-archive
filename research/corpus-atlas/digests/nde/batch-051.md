- Most accounts describe peace, love, and welcoming beings, as in Jvq4sQwAVVw and KBqFTvLbyzY. KBmNmIS7AaY instead describes torture, chained souls, and terror, while K1aw8E8aVKQ begins in a void of "negative sludge" and absence of God.
- Several accounts identify Jesus or God as the source of truth, including K2MVoxvef5M and JynincTZ3y4. KAc64qPQaa4 reports being told that religion does not matter, while JyeHFtg_xio frames the experience as union with all souls, matter, and pure consciousness.
- The archive rates some perceptions relatively strongly, including K-CCneVnkAM at 26, K131c0ABUus at 21, and KBmNmIS7AaY at 20. Many cards explicitly note no verifiable perception, including K-HF4aVNGtU and K1rSlAYCGxk.
- Return is sometimes chosen or accepted, as in JxOVWN0yWFY and K2MVoxvef5M, but is resisted or experienced as forced in K1TrJKxsZO8 and Jvq4sQwAVVw.
