- Many life reviews are compassionate and nonjudgmental, including 2G3ixhMxb2I, but 2Oz98Aoaaf0 presents accusing unknown individuals who say, "You abandoned us when we needed you the most."
- Several accounts identify a Christian God or Jesus, including 2aCQi2Li8wc and 2TB8MIJn2dU, while 2JjydDsDdLY and 2n1Jae9btVM use spiritual experiences to challenge inherited doctrines, including pre-life planning and universal salvation.
