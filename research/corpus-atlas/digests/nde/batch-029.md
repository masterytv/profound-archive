## Themes

- Return as mission or obligation, about 30 of 40. Experiencers are sent back, choose family, or receive unfinished work as the reason to return. Examples: B3dQw3T27Ek, B7vEdwuJBEg.
- Love, peace, and reduced fear of death, about 28 of 40. Positive emotional states and post-experience spiritual reassurance dominate, including accounts with very different triggers. Examples: AtHzYbZt-Ws, BCV0xLbp-AU.
- Life review and moral learning, about 17 of 40. Reviews emphasize empathy, consequences, kindness, or learning to love rather than punishment alone. Examples: B74mhljoluo, BBjSc1YhPVQ.
- Cosmic unity and expanded consciousness, about 20 of 40. Light, energy, ripples, collective consciousness, and interconnectedness recur across NDEs and the OBE account. Examples: B1PtD8YenU4, B63oNa4yxMc.
- Deceased relatives and guides as relational authorities, about 20 of 40. Relatives frequently deliver return messages, while angels, religious figures, and guides provide instruction. Examples: B2hjgIpd-Lw, BED6rta6c6w.
- Transformation toward service, compassion, and spiritual work, about 24 of 40. Many accounts connect the experience to healing, teaching, helping suicidal people, or sharing a mission. Examples: B13XQ_y5TRw, B8415mYtxoM.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Most accounts describe love, peace, or serenity, but some begin in terror, sadness, or hostile environments: B2Ucne6TQDo, B3ovid8P_Ik, B3yfVunO1t0. This contrasts with the explicitly peaceful journeys in AtHzYbZt-Ws and BCV0xLbp-AU.
- Return is usually framed as a choice, yet some accounts describe forced or externally imposed return: B2hjgIpd-Lw, B7vEdwuJBEg, BCzSEtgmTRE. Others emphasize conscious personal choice, including AwOooiuU-ig and B8wSMNftPjQ.
- Religious imagery varies sharply. Jesus, God, Mary, and angels appear in B04jMejJrfk, AtHzYbZt-Ws, and BCzSEtgmTRE, while BC3wcptQGFs emphasizes Buddhist death-bardo concepts and B63oNa4yxMc presents impersonal ripples and universal consciousness.
- Transformation is often lasting, but BBN4IZ8ndS0 says its deep peace and connection faded after a week, while B8wSMNftPjQ reports years of depression and disconnection before integration.
