## Standout accounts

- **npuP7AxBBMs, NDE Reveals The Truth About What Truly Matters In Life**: The card records a 32/32 Greyson score, 10/10 intensity and 15/15 elements, including a life review, future visions and a choice to return motivated by love. It is the clearest high-completeness example of the batch's moral-relational pattern.
- **neYfIZk8Pg8, Man Dies In Car Wreck; Shown Truth About Free Will**: Jeffrey's account combines catastrophic family loss, a deceased wife's appeal to return for their surviving son, a life review and a reported 42 transformation score. It illustrates return as responsibility rather than preference.
- **nrmokEN0thQ, Clinically Dead Man Travels to the Afterlife and Back With a Message From God**: This 15/15 account layers tunnel, light, Jesus, parents, life review and a return message centered on his sister. It strongly represents the deceased-family and purpose themes.
- **nYwsMuZ5R-0, Woman Died During Surgery, Sees Future Beings Beyond Comprehension**: The experiencer describes geometric light, a powerful loving being, a personal contract and a journey through humanity's history and the universe. It expands the usual heaven narrative into cosmology and abstract intelligence.
- **nlufn8scknY, NDE TV Presents Alicia, victim of domestic violence has a horrible demonic Near-Death Experience**: Its sequence includes shadow beings, a demonic figure, a stormy ocean, a child and eventual freedom from fear. It is a major counterpoint to the overwhelmingly peaceful tone.
- **nrv6j2162mc, Woman Died & Learns We Have It Completely Backward**: Denise reports leaving her body about eight times over two days and experiencing universal knowing and freedom from bodily attachment. The repeated exits complicate the idea of one discrete NDE episode.
- **o-CfJIaANJA, Neuroscientist Had Shared Death Experience With Colleague**: Bob's account is a shared-death experience rather than a conventional resuscitation narrative, with a claimed knowing of a friend's death and a later research vocation. It links anomalous experience to scientific inquiry.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Most journeys are peaceful and loving, but nlufn8scknY and nnIF-sScMK0 describe frightening demonic or hellish encounters, while npuP7AxBBMs and nYmODXP7AZg describe complete peace or rescue by light.
- Return is often resisted, as in nni0hJHd5Ls, noDeRq2Wmg8 and nvC6oK4n2tM, but some experiencers actively choose it, including nY2cflYhRvg, nyXYzpjT0UU and npuP7AxBBMs.
- Accounts disagree on whether the experience is singular: nrv6j2162mc reports about eight exits over two days, while nni0hJHd5Ls describes a clinically dead episode without a clearly sequenced narrative.
- Religious figures vary from Jesus and God, as in ne18LuApp8I and nzomaT3h7T0, to gaseous beings, blue-skinned beings and abstract universal consciousness, as in ncMtQ-gEwk4, neuciWsQxp4 and nyXYzpjT0UU.
