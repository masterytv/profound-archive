## Tensions and contradictions

- Most accounts are peaceful and very positive, but iOZ60JhxRbM describes torture and demons, while i8DdlDZhx3Y describes unconditional acceptance without judgment.
- Return is often reluctant, as in iDkhDPzMjYI and i6W8ZxNzaiU, but i6KdwZN0f_4 and iG9pS7gtik0 frame return as an assigned responsibility.
- Some accounts claim verifiable physical observation, including iGgyN7eCRVs and iELND_902U8, while i5qCn4cqqzM and i6W8ZxNzaiU explicitly lack specific veridical claims.
- Life review can be compassionate and nonjudgmental in i8DdlDZhx3Y, but accusatory and punitive in iILRQxElSss and iG_OIq0AVTY.
- Mary Neal's card describes lasting fear reduction and fuller living but assigns Transformation 0, contrasting with the high transformation scores in iELND_902U8 and iOoP2dZ7ksQ.
