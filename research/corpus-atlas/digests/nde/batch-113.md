## Tensions and contradictions

- Most accounts describe peace, love, and benevolent entities, but hK6KX8_k9Tc reports only blackness and neutrality, while hE43IUyyuDM and hKUxQecGlrM report intense light and welcome.
- Return is often framed as a choice, as in hPTqFieiI8g and hDg5pmvAuW0, but other cards describe being forced or instructed back, including hDhYK1ipR4M and hL07_txfcvA.
- Several accounts retain explicit Christian figures, including hGaZ_OmSgdE and hXt0PLubKHs, while hLyPHKMkxXE reports leaving organized religion and adopting a nonreligious spiritual ethic.
- Life review can imply moral consequence, as in hL07_txfcvA and hQL2N6UqXTE, yet hDhYK1ipR4M explicitly presents hellish experiences as ego loops rather than divine punishment.
- Evidential strength does not track experiential richness: hIBGOEcxRyM and hPGRblGsthc receive high ratings, while hKUxQecGlrM has 15 elements but only a low evidential rating.
