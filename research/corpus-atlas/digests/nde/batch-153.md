## Themes

- Peace, love, and relief from bodily pain: about 30 of 40. Peace commonly follows separation from the body or entry into light, including accounts with severe injury or illness (xjf7kMYVipo, y2yd6SqG9pg).
- Choice, summons, or pressure to return: about 28 of 40. Return is framed as unfinished work, family obligation, or a direct instruction (xl0a3lm35pM, xpYnBDyPFJs).
- Life review and moral perspective: about 18 of 40. Reviews involve rapid images, emotional consequences, or seeing events from other people's perspectives (xl8tQrPWwlE, xo4qaSD-Y4E).
- Spiritual beings and religious figures: about 27 of 40. God, Jesus, angels, deceased relatives, and culturally specific figures appear as guides, healers, judges, or companions (xiuSeJvsHcQ, xxi8LRwiGjs).
- Transformation toward purpose and service: about 25 of 40. Accounts describe less fear of death and stronger commitments to family, healing, spirituality, or helping others (xgzQA1WiYwc, y4SMAm-WdaQ).
- Distressing or hellish material: about 5 of 40. These range from suicide-related darkness to elaborate hell visions and predictive catastrophe narratives (xt6HtN6JVTI, xvwvK3aA8OI).

## Tensions and contradictions

- Return is often experienced as a loving choice or assignment, but some accounts describe refusal, coercion, or stern instruction. Bruno says he chose to return for planned life activities, while other cards describe being told to return or physically forced back (xl8tQrPWwlE, xgzQA1WiYwc, y4SMAm-WdaQ).
- The dominant journey tone is peaceful and very positive, but suicide and hell accounts are explicitly distressing and negative (xiuSeJvsHcQ, xxCp0F65_3w; xt6HtN6JVTI, xvwvK3aA8OI, xzqVpSbzMBA).
- Deceased relatives are frequent comforting figures, yet one account presents a deceased relative as neutral and another features a threatening or demonic environment instead of reunion (xpBa6BkY4Nw, xxi8LRwiGjs; xvwvK3aA8OI).
- Veridical strength varies sharply: most cards rate low, while claimed physical observations and medical outcomes receive higher ratings, especially Vinnie Tolman and Anita Moorjani (xo4qaSD-Y4E, xpYnBDyPFJs).
