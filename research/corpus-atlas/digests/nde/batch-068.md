## Tensions and contradictions

- Positive, loving afterlife environments dominate, as in QVGA6xeQhOs and Q_DrclCSBaU, but Qhei7kbTq0U and QmUQFufRj3c describe hell, torment, demons, fire, and moral judgment.
- Many accounts report a tunnel or bright light, including QU2BrV2FqZE and Qd7mxjPtonE, while QiW4rgle2Bk reports only an undifferentiated void and QkEJCM6GId0 reports peaceful limbo with no visuals.
- Return is often forced, as in QWs2qojUFoY and QdBwhOykLro, but Qj1QAP6rf1Q and Qg29aKfNLIY describe an explicit personal choice to return.
- Life review commonly emphasizes compassion and learning, as in Qb9SEatngv8 and Qbvs1ncHcwo, whereas QmUQFufRj3c frames the review through sin, punishment, and religious doctrine.
- Veridical strength varies sharply: QZWYQpJP2d4 and QgFtJYYOHEU receive exceptional ratings, while QYoiJvEmvc8 and QjSxTEAu22U explicitly lack specific veridical perceptions.
