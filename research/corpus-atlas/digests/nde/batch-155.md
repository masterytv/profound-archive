## Tensions and contradictions

- Most cards receive low archive evidential-strength ratings despite vivid, detailed experiences, as in yXfPyJSU2Z0 and yepi-lISuBQ, while yh1EMNV1L7U, yiBDUB4CX2I, and yu9ljcHcu3w receive high or exceptional ratings.
- Return is frequently voluntary or negotiated, as in yj9tGaPaH2I and yjnH4MK5o4k, but other accounts describe forced return or external commands, including yjuplutDPD8 and yqOmoXTJFM4.
- Many journeys are overwhelmingly peaceful, including ypqKfwek8Ws and ynCrGkNmKsI, while ydiu-wrs7SM and yrHWBCYm9kw include fear, darkness, or hostile entities.
- Some accounts present a personal God or Jesus, including yu9ljcHcu3w and yuAHfg2BjOQ, while others describe impersonal unity or consciousness, including ygM8_EQopnQ and ypQRu62s2mE.
- The archive labels some spiritually transformative or dream-like accounts as NDEs, while yaWiPiE5jQs is explicitly an STE and ypQRu62s2mE an OBE.
