## Tensions and contradictions

- The batch repeatedly presents return as imposed or unavoidable, as in "No, it's not your time. Go back" (ASY1W2ftCBY), while other experiencers explicitly choose return for purpose, family, or unfinished work (ASxrHdE2szE, AmIUmDQKnPo).
- Several accounts describe Jesus or God as the central being (ASfXZHniI8A, AgFCJqGzatc), but others distinguish a guide from God (AZ0rR7080bM), encounter Laozi or Daoist figures (ATo0dNbUjDs, AheANcOMtMI), or report no clearly religious figure (AWaM7bhTEQ0).
- Most journeys are positive, yet distress, hell imagery, demonic voices, or fear appears in ARPtgIXecvk, AgFCJqGzatc, and Ad5va6Gckig.
- High reported vividness does not consistently correspond to high evidential strength: AQIVHg2WEnc reports vividness 10/10 but Veridical 10, while AcZi2ppcneg reports vividness 9/10 and Veridical 20. AQIVHg2WEnc, AcZi2ppcneg
