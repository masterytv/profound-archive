## Tensions and contradictions

- Return agency conflicts with forced return. Barbara says, "I made the decision to come back," while other accounts describe being sent back or commanded, including QqNIifpXSQs and R5R5fd9WVz8.
- The afterlife is frequently loving and peaceful, but R25f3zhQ4JM presents a terrifying lake of fire, while R2k3QMArB2Q includes shadow figures that drain energy. These contrast with QrhcApTXwMk and QH9fQwHNEw, where divine or otherworldly encounters are overwhelmingly loving.
- Some accounts claim concrete physical verification, particularly RBsPLFjGnVc, which reports details verified by the husband, and RJ0hTJGwf70, which describes seeing the swimmer from a distance. By contrast, the cards explicitly rate Qntv7t8kBhY and QoSpqr4ohvg as lacking independently verifiable perceptions.
- The archive includes uncertain or nonstandard cases. QytpIhdq8oY and QzBvf9lIfxM have low Greyson scores and notes saying no clear NDE sequence, while QqvrmL8Vxik and QuipTpfyfIA show highly complete, conventional patterns.
