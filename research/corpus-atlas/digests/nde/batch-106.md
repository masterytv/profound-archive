- Return is often presented as a choice, as in ePBzS8hS36k and eRGqeutT9Bw, but other accounts describe forced or externally imposed return, including edXP0pdpkxs and ehBxIRkGDR4.
- The afterlife is usually peaceful and loving, as in eUF14qgTe4w and ekbmd1_sqT0, but fear, judgment, darkness, or distress appear in eQUrxAocfdI and ecIGWaQXS3s.
- Relatives frequently appear as guides, as in eNETJUp8URI and ekmMfwa1LJ4, while eMFoi861dJU instead encounters a version of the self, and e_t-Dl71A7I encounters seven monitors.
