- The dominant pattern is peaceful or loving, but UItXrmtyE28 and UO8oAnjqXlQ describe hellish realms, fear and hostile beings.
- Return is often chosen or accepted, as in U8__UHT8hLI and UJyHra52zgA, but other accounts describe forced or externally imposed return, including U2QZu8pqc0I and UPTgLm8AVyo.
- Religious identity varies sharply: Jesus and Christian salvation dominate some accounts, while Vishnu appears in U8Ae4PMAAqk and theological discussion rejects simple religious framing in UCdzRHPGJzQ.
- High narrative detail does not consistently produce high evidential ratings. UEnE61R_aLI has a rating of 18, while similarly elaborate accounts such as U5ULZ5GUZJY and UJzT5qAoMQU are rated 10 and 12.
- The two Jacob Cooper cards present related but not identical records: U8__UHT8hLI identifies a five-year-old, while UHm7IdnWoN4 identifies a three-year-old and provides a different title and account emphasis.
