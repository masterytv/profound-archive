## Themes

- **Peace, love, and reduced fear of death, about 30 of 40:** Most accounts describe peace, unconditional love, or a transformed attitude toward death, often followed by a return and increased purpose. Examples include 6Oneto4uX7k and 6jQ0419NtTE.
- **Out-of-body observation, about 25 of 40:** Experiencers commonly report observing their bodies, rescuers, hospitals, homes, or accident scenes from above. Particularly detailed examples appear in 6TauEPcTc8g and 6hogP2qF3RI.
- **Knowledge, purpose, and life review, about 20 of 40:** Encounters frequently involve instant knowledge, moral review, or a post-return mission centered on love and service. Examples include 6P9HTBjBPGQ and 6nDqPUmGzrg.
- **Religious and deceased figures, about 20 of 40:** Jesus, God, angels, deceased relatives, guides, and other beings appear across otherwise varied experiences. Examples include 6OQs8YTsq-I and 6iamZOcsm08.
- **Nonstandard experiences alongside classic NDEs, about 8 of 40:** The batch includes an ADC, an STE, a pre-birth account, spontaneous OBEs, meditation, and dreamlike or spiritual-crisis narratives. Examples include 6e-gyRGiwtM and 6gw_dZD1eqI.

## Tensions and contradictions

- **Positive versus distressing journeys:** Most accounts are positive and love-centered, such as 6Oneto4uX7k and 6Mwo20rDISo, while 6lDnttJnT08 and 6jOhjQQZVrs describe fear, hellish imagery, guilt, or darkness before resolution.
- **Choice versus compulsion in returning:** Some experiencers explicitly choose to return, as in 6PLpqoLeBrs and 6aAowKjBok8, while others are told to return or are pulled back, as in 6OQs8YTsq-I and 6jN-xtjkxs4.
- **Religious specificity versus nonreligious unity:** Jesus, God, and Muhammad appear as distinct figures in 6iamZOcsm08 and 6oA1G4FzJA4, whereas 6ZSULlSKEuo and 6dBHDTA2tb4 emphasize impersonal light, energy, or universal consciousness.
- **High evidential ratings versus internal experience:** Specific claimed observations support high ratings in 6cviPcIr3GM and 6TauEPcTc8g, while 6hqqitOzdrk explicitly lacks independently verifiable perceptions.
