## Tensions and contradictions

- **Positive versus hellish journeys:** Most accounts are positive and love-centered, such as g5m2HJax0a8 and gE69yRBI7wI, while gAJShDqxM1E and gCfmw3dZjIk describe fear, demons, and torture.
- **Choice versus forced return:** Some experiencers explicitly choose return, including g70YVRtQa-Y and g8yVD6vZFp4, while others are pulled or sent back, including g5fMCNqt5hk and gC1YX43X9Hc.
- **Veridicality is uneven:** gEcuF8THaow, gD1_DYLr1Z8, and g70YVRtQa-Y receive high or exceptional ratings for physical perceptions, whereas spiritual-download accounts such as g5m2HJax0a8 and gBdlZr3lls0 receive low ratings.
- **NDE classification is unstable:** gBLw-H1Evgg and g4iR6inYdBA are OBEs, while g3yQGY61eFc and gIp8wWgMDsc are SDEs, yet all are presented alongside conventional NDE narratives.
