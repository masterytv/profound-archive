## Tensions and contradictions

- Return is sometimes voluntary, as in choosing to come back for family or compassion, but sometimes imposed or physically abrupt: p91uIInrdFk and pAooyPh-kyE versus oyHX9Y5NmYs and p2DZ_5HYKps.
- The afterlife is often characterized by peace and love, yet several accounts include hell, terror, or hostile entities: p0RE4ImKYBw, p6cfYVUiZGg versus p2Qr3ulk-5o, pG9Xf_Sl7Mg.
- Some narratives present one culturally specific divine figure, while others describe a plural or universal religious reality: p9ZggRgHFS8 and p2j-CyhviTk versus pICciopQs7w.
- Veridical confidence varies sharply. The archive rates oyHX9Y5NmYs, pAooyPh-kyE, pB_isM4-9Fg, and pIoIhgfDpVc relatively highly, while many accounts explicitly lack independently verifiable perceptions, including p0RE4ImKYBw and p91uIInrdFk.
- Aftereffects are not uniformly beneficial or immediate: p3aAk8AKRQg describes decades of isolation and alcohol problems, while pG24QSaSocg discusses no transformation aftereffect.
