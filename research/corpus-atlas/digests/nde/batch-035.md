## Themes
- Return as purpose or obligation, about 27 of 40. Experiencers are told, or decide, that they must return to family, service, or unfinished work. Examples: DN-bhUt14zA, DVhyV771NGY.
- Love, peace, and acceptance, about 30 of 40. Positive accounts repeatedly describe unconditional love, relief from fear, and a welcoming realm. Examples: DANVCZJrSwY, DRc430cTkQk.
- Out-of-body observation, about 28 of 40. Leaving the body, watching resuscitation, or viewing the physical scene is common, though evidential strength varies sharply. Examples: DAUBS-83Lbo, DEeXXnu6u0g.
- Life review and moral perspective, about 18 of 40. Reviews often involve seeing consequences through other people's perspectives rather than punishment alone. Examples: DJ7y4dfedhM, DVhyV771NGY.
- Spiritual transformation, about 30 of 40. Accounts report reduced fear of death, increased compassion, and a stronger sense of spiritual purpose. Examples: DPM0lmiOKbA, DYUytjRPV4s.
- Religious imagery alongside nonsectarian unity, about 20 of 40. Jesus, biblical figures, saints, guides, light beings, and impersonal cosmic oneness coexist across the batch. Examples: DMp_W7ht1rk, DANVCZJrSwY.

## Standout accounts
- DEeXXnu6u0g, "He Died and Was Shown Why We Exist and Earth's Purpose - Vinney Tolman NDE." The card combines a 13-element experience, a life review, a guide explicitly distinguished from Jesus, and a relatively high Veridical rating of 21. It illustrates purpose, moral review, and the recurring claim that Earth is a school.
- DAUBS-83Lbo, "Barbara Bartolome's NDE, 'I Must be Dead'." This is the batch's clearest verification-oriented account, with Veridical 26 and a detailed observation of medical staff during cardiac arrest. - DN-bhUt14zA, "It Will Leave You Speechless - Man Dies & Is Shown Truth About Heaven." It contains all 15 detected elements, including bodily observation, tunnel, life review, deceased relatives, and cosmic unity. It illustrates a highly complete, strongly positive template.
- DRc430cTkQk, "Man Dies, Discovers Ultimate Truth About Our Soul's Purpose on Earth, Consciousness & Oneness." The account presents multiple lives, other planets, a life review, and merging with a loving light. It illustrates cosmic unity and expansive metaphysical interpretation.
- DB4LIhewdGk, "I Saw My Dad Getting A Beat Down By Demons In Hell." This is an unusually distressing, drug-associated account involving a deceased father, demonic figures, torture, and a hellish realm. It illustrates the batch's minority pattern of fear, judgment, and spiritual struggle.
- DXQh28N7eis, "The near death experience of Landon Dennis." Emotional pain and fear precede a beautiful field, deceased relatives, prayer, and a major post-experience life change, with Transformation 40. It illustrates recovery, addiction, family connection, and purpose.
- DXVtrtWBD9I, "SHARED DEATH EXPEREINCE: Transition, Life Review, Afterlife Reunion & Bilocation." A living witness describes accompanying a dying person and deceased relative into light while remaining aware in the hospital room. It illustrates shared experience, reunion, and bilocation.

## Tensions and contradictions
- Most accounts describe a loving, peaceful transition, but DB4LIhewdGk, DL4Sa5ycl4g, and DOilcQClqsI report fear, hell-like settings, or hostile entities.
- Return is often framed as imposed by guides or beings, as in DNA2wbUFVi0, DVhyV771NGY, and DYxmKZxnj1I, while DKU-075i-uY, DM6_S8WU4y8, and DQnzMXJMDiI emphasize personal choice.
- Religious figures are central in DAM7WKa3X0I, DMp_W7ht1rk, and DM6_S8WU4y8, but DANVCZJrSwY rejects organized religion and DRc430cTkQk emphasizes nondual unity.
- Rich phenomenology does not guarantee evidential strength: DN-bhUt14zA has 15 elements but Veridical 12, while DAUBS-83Lbo has only 7 elements but Veridical 26.
