## Themes
- Return as assignment, obligation or unfinished work, about 30 of 40. Experiencers are sent back for children, family, healing or a mission, as in u-2Kukqqqko and uDM2iKenKh8.
- Love, peace and reduced fear of death, about 27 of 40. Calm or unconditional love frequently replaces bodily distress and persists afterward, especially u8Xsic0h0EE and uA6XesPxA0k.
- Life review as relational learning, about 20 of 40. Reviews emphasize emotional impact on others rather than external punishment, as in u9O_LnT6eJU and u8cthvWidVw.
- Out-of-body observation, about 25 of 40. Many accounts place consciousness above an injured or medically treated body, including uA6XesPxA0k and uGMtd3YN3B0.
- Spiritual transformation and purpose, about 25 of 40. The most repeated after-effects are spiritual awareness, changed attitude toward death, compassion, and new direction, including u3WqNlnPNCU and uIELvuJ1288.

## Tensions and contradictions
- Return is sometimes a voluntary choice, as in u-2Kukqqqko and u7BcFqGcdlA, but sometimes forced by a guide, boundary or bodily intervention, as in u0Mngi-Bvr4 and u8cthvWidVw.
- Most accounts describe love, peace or welcome, including u8Xsic0h0EE and u9O_LnT6eJU, while u3cJK0gx9KU describes a barren planet surrounded by desperate figures requesting water.
- Life review is often compassionate and relational in u8cthvWidVw and u9O_LnT6eJU, but u3n1-xjaU80 presents souls changing color and undergoing a more cosmic, instructional process.
- High Greyson scores do not guarantee strong archive evidence: uGdVYqvd0q0 has Greyson 30 and Veridical 9, while uGMtd3YN3B0 has Greyson 30 and Veridical 24.
- Some experiences follow medical crisis or injury, such as u8cthvWidVw, while uLyjsiAeQqk and uK9ChOaBlkQ are spiritually framed episodes without a clearly documented medical crisis.
