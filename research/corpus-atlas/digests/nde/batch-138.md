## Themes

- Love, peace, and reduced fear of death, about 30 of 40. These are explicit in accounts ranging from the void to encounters with divine beings, including rVTwY7-7kNo and rZb-saZC858.
- Spiritual transformation and a new life mission, about 25 of 40. Experiencers commonly report service, testimony, healing, or teaching after the event, as in rgvjY7UtaN4 and rihZHGMJeWE.
- Out-of-body perception and nonordinary sensory vividness, about 25 of 40. The cards repeatedly describe observing the body, expanded perception, or reality feeling more real, including rR6JUP5OoGo and rkqAM9JZoSA.
- Life review, downloaded knowledge, and moral consequence, about 18 of 40. Reviews range from panoramic evaluation to lessons about compassion and others' perspectives, as in rR6JUP5OoGo and rc2WYBblvOw.
- Religious figures and personalized theology, about 15 of 40. God, Jesus, the Holy Spirit, angels, and a Creator appear, but some accounts frame the source as love, consciousness, or an unknown being, including rUxPWg5II5Y and rlMfY_l8aC4.
- Return as a decision, assignment, or obligation, about 20 of 40. Some choose to return, while others are told that it is not their time or that they have work remaining, including riaULQA99iI and rfFCvYLImhs.

## Tensions and contradictions

- The dominant journey is peaceful and loving, but ragcEDleVcU reports assault, terror, pain, and demonic denial of God, while rVTwY7-7kNo describes the void as home-like, loving, and peaceful.
- Return is often framed as chosen, including riaULQA99iI and r_120dXGKCA, but other accounts describe forced or externally required return, including rR6JUP5OoGo and rhMJ37sTExo.
- Christian interpretations are prominent in rUxPWg5II5Y and riQ6wK_NksU, whereas rc2WYBblvOw explicitly presents a life review without religious elements and rSxwPkr9j7Y describes a higher intellect without a fixed religious identity.
- High Greyson scores do not consistently correspond to strong evidential claims. riuoB-pX4Ok has 30/32 with Veridical 10, while ragcEDleVcU has 20/32 with Veridical 21.
