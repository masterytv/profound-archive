## Tensions and contradictions
- Most accounts describe benevolent light, love and peace, while tIwCe4Ukync describes a strongly negative hell journey with demons and torment. tZgb_vg0Q1U also reports hostile demonic beings, but places Jesus' rescue at the center.
- Return is frequently imposed by a guide or boundary, as in tQUgd7n9RIQ and tQovIouLceM, but other accounts explicitly choose to return because of family or purpose, as in tKCP3dUZYzs and tPfUy6vUQPo.
- The archive's evidential ratings vary substantially. tReEhEoe79c reports specific medical perceptions and scores 20, while tJke2An_hnw presents future disaster visions but scores 10 and notes no independently verifiable perception.
- Several accounts describe Jesus as radiant or luminous, including tPul5D1RSrU, while tcbI5WuwAas describes Jesus, Buddha and Mary in ordinary human forms. The shared religious meaning does not require a shared visual form.
