## Tensions and contradictions

- Most journeys are peaceful and loving, but Jason reports hellish torture and judgment, while Kat moves through both heaven and hell (bYcuYsKiO8Q, baquIFSE2h0, bllBdgGJmik).
- Return is often resisted, yet some experiencers choose it or accept it willingly. Dax, Pam, and the lightning experiencer beg to remain, while Jonathan, Masat, and Robert decide to return (bYcuYsKiO8Q, bnZ2KyfwAuw, bbQK5rOMvF0, bcpvG_H3t0o, bkq8NpXpuBs, bZI5iGIDp6Y).
- Religious identity moves in opposite directions: Kat changes from atheist to Christian, while the lightning experiencer becomes more spiritual and less religious (bllBdgGJmik, bbQK5rOMvF0).
- Veridical strength varies sharply. The cards assign Exceptional or High ratings to detailed medical or environmental perceptions, but many similarly vivid accounts contain no veridical claims (b_4prfyjP00, bbQK5rOMvF0, bmnQDUEn7fI, bVCccQgD-Hs, bbZ-dMSIg7M).
