- Return is sometimes described as a voluntary decision, including choosing children or family, as in Cg1WFbFlyDY and CkRFucDyeNQ. Other accounts describe force, interruption or an authoritative command, as in CUnqYuT-XCU and Cl4hMnKYPlU.
- Most journeys are peaceful and loving, but distressing or infernal content appears in CQQTDseHp1w and CbS1au2tx-Y.
- High phenomenological richness does not consistently track evidential strength: CSY3oXXVaf0 and CdvRq3vZ9eY each have 13 or 14 elements but Veridical ratings of 11 and 7, while CVcBCUy3ZDw has only five elements but a rating of 23.
