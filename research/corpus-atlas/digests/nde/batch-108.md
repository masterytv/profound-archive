- Light, peace and unconditional love, about 30 of 40, appear as the dominant emotional and sensory pattern, including fKCEodb2pzM and fS8ffJuOXJM.
- Return as a choice, instruction or imposed necessity, about 30 of 40, frequently linked to children, missions or unfinished work, as in fLMcbbsQAw0 and fZOi4qUclkI.

## Standout accounts
- fL02muEVBwA, "Doctor Dies and Meets God On The Other Side, Is Sent Back With A Message (Near Death Experience) | Dorothy Shelton": One of the most element-rich accounts, with 14 of 15 detected elements and a direct moral question from God about love. It illustrates life review, unconditional love and transformation.
- fLPmAA9AZTI, "What This Man Saw Beyond Death Will Change How You See Life - Profound NDE | Life & Beyond": A 10/10 experience combining tunnel travel, musical flowers, multiple beings, cosmic knowledge and a world-history tablet. It illustrates the batch's strongest synthesis of wonder, cosmic unity and purpose.
- fRroXDCfjQM, "NEAR-DEATH EXPERIENCE: NDE. Can you Survive Death? I lost my FEAR with Jose Hernandez | Passion Harvest Podcast": An atheist describes CPR observation, a feminine presence, living colors and unity with nature, followed by a loss of fear. - fQdlDDVYWKo, "NDE TV Presents , Pegi, 2 Near- Death Experiences, ages 5 and 25, and several spiritual experiences.": This mixed account includes childhood distress, telepathic observation of family activity and a relatively high Veridical rating of 14. - fXATJz-vQKU, "Round Trip Death #203 - David's Near Death Experience": David's account ranges from pyramids and planetary energies to reincarnation, Gaia and Jesus' message to love one another. It illustrates expansive cosmology and unusually broad knowledge claims.
- fTYtZ9uVnAo, "CHILLING DETAILS: I Was Abducted by Aliens HUNDREDS of Times...": The NDE centers on paralysis, an apparent medical error, observation through walls and two unseen orderlies. It illustrates the archive's strongest overlap between NDE, anomalous perception and evidential claims.

## Tensions and contradictions
- Return is sometimes welcomed as a mission, as in fZOi4qUclkI and fbep_EtYB-M, but sometimes experienced as frustrating or devastating, as in fKCEodb2pzM and fNh250aop50.
- The afterlife is often described as peaceful and loving, as in fS8ffJuOXJM and fL02muEVBwA, yet fear, judgment or distress appears in fQdlDDVYWKo, f_J1x87N4BU and fbq8jJ2g2rE.
- Religious figures are central for some accounts, including Jesus or God in fMtPfUyR4OA and fdfc8gdNAQ4, while others describe an androgynous Creator or nonreligious energy, as in fOZQiDkzM4g and fRroXDCfjQM.
- Life review is prominent in fM3lzql-zHc and fd4qxHrp9-U, but explicitly absent in f_J1x87N4BU and not central to fTYtZ9uVnAo.
- Evidential strength varies from low, such as fJ_tWl90zHM, to high, such as fZOi4qUclkI, showing a substantial divide between subjective revelation and claimed verifiable perception.

## Book-angle sparks
- "The return problem": why do some experiencers resist return while others accept it as a mission, especially when children or unfinished work are invoked? (fKCEodb2pzM, fLMcbbsQAw0, fZOi4qUclkI)
- "Love without religion": compare Christian encounters with Creator, energy and cosmic-unity accounts. (fMtPfUyR4OA, fOZQiDkzM4g, f_FaFTHbBRU)
- "When heaven judges": examine moral evaluation, shame, compassion and the possibility of distress within otherwise loving journeys. (fbq8jJ2g2rE, fQdlDDVYWKo, fL02muEVBwA)
- "Evidence at the edge": contrast high-rated perceptions with accounts explicitly described as subjective or unverifiable. (fZOi4qUclkI, fTYtZ9uVnAo, fJ_tWl90zHM)
