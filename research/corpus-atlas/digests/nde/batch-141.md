## Tensions and contradictions

- Most accounts describe a welcoming, loving realm, but sjvD1FvIhdo reports hell, deceptive entities, and torment, while seG1D8wAwsI also begins with frightening figures before reaching kind beings of light.
- Many experiencers do not want to return, including sS68CbEogTs, slZvbZJvHJU, and srQxFzbiYuQ, while others actively choose return for children or unfinished work, including sRvOATE-84M, sSI2u9hYCgQ, and sj9FTWZelZU.
- Religious imagery is central in some accounts, including Jesus or God in sh1jUjgSI_8 and seR6Wa0qfbw, but other accounts describe formless light or universal consciousness without a named deity, including sUgMPIFHPmQ and snfh9y7REhM.
- High vividness does not consistently imply high evidential strength: sSzHyg8V3oo and spkZ9jAuets report vivid experiences with higher evidential ratings, while many vivid spiritual narratives, including sSAn9l7xHpM and sssQ41nXxc0, receive low ratings.
