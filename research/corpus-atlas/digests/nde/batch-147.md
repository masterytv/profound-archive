## Standout accounts
- upmgXkXBKII, "Jason Janas: The Abundance of Love." This card records all 15 standard elements, a Greyson score of 30/32, and a transformation score of 43. Its combination of God, Jesus, unconditional love, ICU observation, and a later OBE illustrates the batch's maximal theological and transformational pattern.
- urnNTJaWSzo, "How I met my deceased grandfather AFTER I died - NDE / Near Death Experience." The grandfather appears as a recognizable deceased relative, gives a direct purpose statement, and the card assigns Veridical 19, one of the batch's stronger evidential ratings. It illustrates deceased-family encounters and return-for-purpose narratives.
- uvlWJ8Sa4rg, "Firefighter with PTSD Dies In Motorcycle Crash; Makes A Deal With God During NDE." The account combines severe trauma, an explicit bargain about returning without paralysis, and a claimed medically surprising recovery. Its Veridical score of 23 is the highest among the individual accounts in this batch.
- v6Cqy64E4Ho, "A Compilation of NDE's From Various Cultures (Part 1)." This account receives 15/15 elements, with every listed element at high confidence, and Veridical 25. Its operating-room observation, tunnel, life review, and angelic figures represent the fullest standard-element profile in the batch.
- v-jqDHMQp_s, "Drowned In the Sea - Sarah Friederich's Shared Death Experience." Unlike most cards, the experiencer survives while witnessing her husband's death, reviewing both lives and receiving a message that she has a task. It illustrates shared death experience, grief, and acceptance rather than a conventional personal NDE.
- v4w1m5PqwZM, "Woman Dies and Sees ALL of Hell - The Horrifying Truth." Despite the title, the card describes a loving, nonjudgmental life review, Jesus and Buddha teaching children, and a library. It illustrates how hell language can coexist with a positive journey and universalist moral framing.

## Tensions and contradictions
- Positive, peaceful destinations dominate, but explicit distress appears in v-ICN2NYHtI, where the experiencer describes hell, fear, regret, and a life review of wrongdoing. A mixed journey with demonic entities also appears in vFINxFxEbQs, while v4w1m5PqwZM presents Jesus and Buddha in a benevolent teaching environment.
- Return is sometimes freely chosen, as in v9wqDJi6tFs and vG6tKuNx0jo, but elsewhere it is imposed or strongly directed by beings, as in urwAKQKvXuA and vBycNsnhc7Q.
- Some cards describe detailed physical-world perceptions and receive high Veridical ratings, especially v2S2NUw_WkI and vBihF8HeXo8. Others explicitly lack physical-world veridical claims despite elaborate spiritual content, including upKCqj2QH2E and v0ipPryUuZc.
- Transformation ranges from 43 in upmgXkXBKII and 38 in v0ipPryUuZc to zero in vG6tKuNx0jo, showing that vividness and NDE intensity do not guarantee reported after-effects.
