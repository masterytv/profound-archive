## Tensions and contradictions
- Most accounts describe love, peace and a positive journey, including mJ-Cgdi6vLHY and mEI5iN1vcPE, but mI8YHUVR5Hw describes fear, physical trauma and no transformation, while lwg9iAggyww begins with terror and resistance to death.
- Return is commonly welcomed or accepted as a duty to family, as in lwkOnahGlsc and mHfdGhcbg4o, but m62ZqfT1wRM says the experiencer wanted to remain gone, and m3vB2J-BH6Y says the experiencer resisted returning despite being told it was necessary.
- Religious imagery is explicit in lvranBrf7a, mAL12iR9dMc and mJUfF9po7m4, while lyYKkkyqYZM and m46vcdcNiQ8 frame reality more as consciousness, unity or collective mind.
- Many cards describe classic NDE sequences despite low evidential strength, such as m-JCXOUIuBM and m8-e9SCBXIA, while mL3nT0cAZ2g and m46vcdcNiQ8 receive much stronger evidential ratings tied to specific perceptions.
