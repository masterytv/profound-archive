## Standout accounts
- jD4tADRGKkk, "I died and saw my family in paradise": A highly scored account, Greyson 30/32 and Veridical 23, combining operating-room observation, deceased relatives, a life review and a Book of Life. It illustrates evidential claims alongside the recurring family-reunion theme.
- jPqMsmvgE3w, "Leonard Rogers: NDE of a Three-Year-Old Father": The account includes a future son seen during a life review and a reported duration of "42 years," despite the experiencer being three. It illustrates unusual time distortion and childhood NDE material.
- jS-ngtw639A, "MINDBLOWING NDE! I Met my Home and Family From a Parallel Life!": The experience includes Yeshua, a water planet and a remembered parallel-life family, while still following the familiar love, mission and return structure.
- jXZLIzqA5cQ, "Reverend Nigel's 2 NDEs": Two experiences, drowning and severe illness, are presented together, with a yellow tunnel, Book of Life, heaven, hell and Jesus. It illustrates how repeated NDEs can produce layered cosmologies.
- j_4WAd3fFEY, "When We Die, There is Always Someone There to Pick Us Up": A grandmother guides the experiencer through relatives, immense light, galaxies and a realm described as containing all existence. It strongly illustrates deceased-family mediation and expansive knowledge claims.
- jcx7kliqzlI, "Woman Dies Of Heat Stroke; Was Shown How To Overcome Negative Thoughts": The life review focuses on frozen moments of judgmental thought and ends with instructions to repair four relationships. It illustrates the batch's shift from abstract doctrine toward interpersonal ethics.
- je2ZKXn2Fdc, "I DIED and was taken to Hell by a Demon": This is the clearest negative outlier, featuring torture, demons, hell and severe distress before a transition to heaven. It illustrates the minority hell-journey pattern.

## Tensions and contradictions
- Most accounts describe peace, love and welcome, while je2ZKXn2Fdc describes a demon, torture and "no hope"; jXZLIzqA5cQ also includes a glimpse of hell's evil amid an otherwise positive journey.
- Return is sometimes forced or commanded, as in jT31XqZgzv0 and jccCDsrYKIA, but other accounts emphasize agency, including jZfbO2XFshY and jNBAu-0BtmE.
- Divine identity varies from Jesus or God, as in jT31XqZgzv0 and jMiRU4HUWik, to a Lao Tzu-like figure in jCzfIZQcc9w, a Catholic saint in jFUD58d4bJw, and a nonreligious masculine entity in jYXjxZVDWxI.
- The archive includes strong veridical ratings for jD4tADRGKkk and jPqMsmvgE3w, but many highly vivid accounts explicitly lack verifiable physical-world claims, including jFUD58d4bJw and jFwBmP2ymWg.
