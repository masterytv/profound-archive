## Themes

- Love, peace, and acceptance as the dominant emotional register, about 30 of 40, including cardiac arrest, drowning, overdose, illness, and accident accounts. Examples: SmLMEJQlHG8, SnFdfEvy6L4.
- Out-of-body perception and separation from the physical body, about 25 of 40, often followed by heightened or extraordinary senses. Examples: SmbpJmoXVPY, SzOJHe-BoAk.
- Life review as moral perspective-taking rather than punishment, about 18 of 40. Several accounts describe feeling other people's pain or seeing consequences from multiple viewpoints: T1ZdD7KEZXk, SzOJHe-BoAk.
- Deceased relatives, soul families, or familiar companions as guides and reassurers, about 20 of 40. Examples: SpyB-ElkeRk, T-_nrre93nQ.
- Return as a mission, choice, or obligation, about 25 of 40. The recurring after-effect is reduced fear of death plus greater purpose, compassion, or spiritual awareness: StXWV6Z-hvI, T5giGHYv4kU.

## Tensions and contradictions

- Return is sometimes experienced as a welcome choice, as in "You can stay or you can come with me" (SpyB-ElkeRk), but elsewhere as refusal, compulsion, or a mission imposed by guides: "You can't stay here. It's not your time yet." (T5giGHYv4kU). SmbpJmoXVPY also records wanting to stay while being forced back.
- Life review is frequently loving and nonjudgmental, with "no judgment, no hatred, only love" (SviA0uvGUq0), but T-CQniGNTb0 and T69fFcY-Puc frame the afterlife through guilt, torment, demons, and hell.
- Spiritual authority is predominantly Christian, especially Jesus or God (SnFdfEvy6L4, Swy1fxaqWpQ), but other accounts center Shiva and Rishis (T6fEH_hoLh0), a dragon (SmSE8ewHfaw), or a twin sister (T-_nrre93nQ).
- Veridical strength does not track narrative intensity: highly elaborate cosmic accounts receive low ratings (SnP4cbu3WxE, SsdHH5aOlus), while T6fEH_hoLh0 receives Exceptional Evidential Strength despite only five detected elements.
