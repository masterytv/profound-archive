## Tensions and contradictions

- Most journeys are peaceful and loving, as in rDb1ozw61Bw and r5oj6vNXDvM, but rH2pqLQU22c and rNrqa7CaZEE describe hellish realms, torment, fear, and hostile entities.
- Return is often framed as personal choice, as in rHWBZSfWTVs and r2sIRtE9DRI, while other accounts describe forced or commanded return, as in r3no_PDEDWk and rClF0LAEt5k.
- Tunnel travel is prominent in r27tdq6tbK4 and rA8Zua9Dw8s, but rHWBZSfWTVs says the experiencer went directly to the light, and rKMHsB3Z--8 explicitly describes no tunnel.
- Deceased relatives commonly appear as recognizable family members, as in r27tdq6tbK4 and r8tByph631k, yet r2f6TAE7ne presents deceased sisters as "short blue angels," while rBV3lnZiNjc includes Civil War soldiers and a message-bearing child.
