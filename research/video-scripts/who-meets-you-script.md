Project Profound long-form video (12:35) plus a 60-second vertical cut. Topic #1 from `TOPICS.md` in this folder. Every archive quote below was re-checked against `nde_vids.raw_timestamped_subtitles` with `scripts/video-scripts/verify_clips.py`; the clip table is also in `who-meets-you-clips.csv`. ## A. Package

**Logline.** We searched 6,454 near-death experiences to find out who meets people when they die. Almost a third say it was family, most often a grandparent; then the archive shows the cases that a "the brain shows you who you expect" explanation can't cover.

**Target viewer.** Adults 35-70, mostly women, who have lost someone or are caring for someone dying; spiritual-but-not-religious or loosely religious; already watching NDE interviews (Diary Of A CEO, Coming Home, The Other Side NDE) and wanting something that sums up many accounts instead of one. Second audience: curious skeptics who want numbers and the hard cases.

**Title options**

1. We Searched 6,454 Near-Death Experiences: This Is Who Meets You When You Die *(recommended)*
2. Who Is Waiting for You? 1,972 People Who Died Describe the Same Moment
3. Your Grandmother Is the Most Likely Person to Meet You When You Die
4. They Met Someone in Death They Didn't Know Had Died

**Thumbnail options (4 words or fewer)**

1. Close-up of an older hand reaching for a younger hand, warm backlight, dark background. Text: **WHO MEETS YOU?** Small data tag: "1,972 of 6,454". *(recommended)*
2. Faded family photograph, one face lit, the rest dim. Text: **SHE WAS WAITING**
3. Tom, half-lit, holding an old photo toward camera. Text: **NOT EXPECTED**

```sql
select count(*) as videos,
       count(*) filter (where jsonb_array_length(coalesce(a.entities->'encounters','[]'::jsonb)) > 0) as met_being,

from nde_vids v join nde_analysis a on a.video_id = v."videoId"
where v."isNde"::text = 'clear_nde';
```

## Decisions I made

- **Topic.** "Who comes to meet you" scored highest (9.30) and fits the current Diary Of A CEO deathbed-visions wave; deathbed visions themselves became the cold open because the archive is survivor testimony.
- **Runtime 12:35** (inside 10-14) so the hard cases in Act 3 get room; the Eben clip lands at 9:00, 71% through, close to the requested two-thirds point.
- **Honesty beat.** Kept the actuarial objection on camera (grandparents lead because they die first). It costs 30 seconds and it is what makes Act 3 an argument instead of a list.
- **Clip choice.** Preferred experiencers speaking on camera over channels that narrate written accounts, and the IANDS uploads for Anita and Eben (one non-profit permission covers two of the biggest names).
- **David Parker.** Kept despite not being an NDE, because it answers the grieving viewer's next question ("are they waiting, or stuck?"), and labelled it plainly.
- **Split Debra's clip** into 2A and 2B with a 2-second photo insert, to avoid putting two garbled caption words ("I smelter", "daa") into on-screen subtitles.
- **Times.** Given to the second in mm:ss as asked, with exact seconds in the CSV; in/out are set from caption-line timing and need a waveform trim.
- **SQL in section E** is the equivalent of what the Python stats script computes; it was not run, because this session reads the database only through PostgREST.
- **Project Profound URLs** follow the site's `/video/[id]` route (`src/app/video/[id]/page.tsx`); the live site isn't reachable from this sandbox, so the pages were not opened.
