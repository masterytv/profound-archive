## Lessons Learned & Best Practices
- **Video-level vs. Encounter-level Separation:** `uap_analysis` tracks video-level metadata (e.g. programs mentioned, classification status), whereas `uap_encounters` tracks individual witness testimonies. In comparisons of raw phenomenology (entities, emotions, physical effects), `uap_encounters` is the correct source of truth.
- **Unique Video Count Calculation:** When querying encounter-level tables for high-level dashboard summaries, compute the unique video count (`Set(row.video_id)`) rather than counting raw rows, to avoid double-counting multi-encounter videos.
